# GTM niche and region for a Peruvian K-12 EdTech (WhatsApp, AI that surfaces and assesses student thinking for teachers)

Research date: 2026-10-17. 15 tool calls. Notes on method: the WebSearch tool returns model-written summaries of the pages it finds. Claims marked "(snippet)" came from those summaries and were not checked against the full page. Fetches of ecosistemastartup.com were blocked, and the INEI PDF came back as binary I could not read.

## 1. Competitor and reference landscape: which niches are taken

### Takeaway
No Peruvian player found so far assesses students' reasoning process over WhatsApp for teachers. Entiendo.ai personalizes content formats (PDF to video, audio and games) in private chain schools. Numeral does adaptive secondary math in La Libertad. uDocz with the World Bank runs an AI math tutor plus vocational guidance for 5th-year secondary students in Lima public schools. Code en mi cole teaches coding in Arequipa, and Bilingües teaches English. The nearest overlaps are "secondary math" (Numeral, uDocz/World Bank) and "AI in private chains" (Entiendo.ai at Saco Oliveros).

### Cited Findings
**Entiendo.ai**
- A Peruvian education-AI startup. Its platform turns PDFs and slide decks into videos, audio, games and interactive activities, adapted to each student's profile — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/entiendo-ai-edtech-peruana-con-ia-entra-a-venture-ready/) (snippet; fetch blocked)
- Founders are Raúl Alcántara (MIT graduate) and Oscar Clemente (graduate of the COAR, Peru's high-performance public schools) — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=832344); [Panamericana](https://panamericana.pe/tecnologia/473863-conoce-talento-peruano-innova-inteligencia-artificial) (snippet)
- It was co-financed by ProInnóvate (Ministerio de la Producción) and "is currently used in Saco Oliveros schools" — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=832344); [KCH Comunicación](https://kchcomunicacion.com/?p=532290) (snippet)
- It was selected for Venture Ready: Peru to U.S. (Golden Circle, the US State Department and the US Embassy). The program gives training, mentoring and contacts but no capital — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/entiendo-ai-startup-peruana-de-ia-educativa-viaja-a-ee-uu/) (snippet)
- UNVERIFIED: target ages, grant amount, pricing, user counts, and the scope of the Saco Oliveros pilot (how many campuses, which grades). The search summary said no source gave funding or user figures.

**Numeral (La Libertad)**
- An adaptive math platform for secondary students that "prioritizes logical understanding over memorization" through guided exercises. Founded in 2017 as a traditional education project, it went digital in 2022 under Axel Juárez. It has reached 200+ students in La Libertad, Piura, Cajamarca and Lima — [ProInnóvate gob.pe, May 2026](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/proinnovate/noticias/1393067-region-la-libertad-startup-presenta-plataforma-digital-que-refueza-ensenanza-de-las-matematicas) (snippet)
- It is one of three edtechs with non-refundable StartUp Perú seed funding that PRODUCE showcased in Sept 2026. The article gives no amounts and names no founders — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/tres-edtech-peruanas-financiadas-por-proinnovate-en-2026/)
- No source found mentions WhatsApp or B2B sales to schools.

**Bilingües**
- A 100% virtual English school using games, challenges and rewards. StartUp Perú funded it; no age band stated — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/tres-edtech-peruanas-financiadas-por-proinnovate-en-2026/)

**Code en mi cole (Arequipa)**
- Teaches schoolchildren programming by having them build video games, and trains teachers in computational thinking, AI and coding. StartUp Perú funded it — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/tres-edtech-peruanas-financiadas-por-proinnovate-en-2026/)

**uDocz and the World Bank ("Eligiendo Mi Camino")**
- An AI math tutor combined with a vocational-guidance assistant, for about 4,500 5th-year secondary students (final year, roughly ages 16-17) in 85 Lima public schools, running since March 2026. Partners listed are uDocz, Anthropic and Microsoft. The challenge it addresses: only 13% of secondary students reach the expected level in math — [World Bank results, 2 Jul 2026 (ES)](https://www.bancomundial.org/es/results/2026/07/02/artificial-intelligence-in-action-in-latin-america-s-schools-evidence-from-peru)
- Conflicting scope figures. A Feb 2026 World Bank brief cited 110 public schools, 6,500 students and 400 trained teachers in an RCT — [World Bank brief](https://www.bancomundial.org/es/brief/2026/02/25/eligiendo-mi-camino) (snippet). An April 2026 press piece said 6,600+ students — [Descubre.vc](https://www.descubre.vc/noticia/udocz-y-el-banco-mundial-lanzan-tutores-de-ia-para-estudiantes-en-per-2026-04-18) (snippet). Treat 85 schools / 4,500 students (Jul 2026) as the latest figure.
- DRELM (Lima's regional education directorate) provides school access and curriculum alignment, and UPC provides teacher training — [Descubre.vc](https://www.descubre.vc/noticia/udocz-y-el-banco-mundial-lanzan-tutores-de-ia-para-estudiantes-en-per-2026-04-18) (snippet; the Jul 2026 World Bank page does not name DRELM or UPC)
- A related RCT in 390 Lima schools found more active AI use among trained teachers. AI-based classroom observation matched trained human observers. Minedu trained 6,000+ school leaders — [World Bank, Jul 2026](https://www.bancomundial.org/es/results/2026/07/02/artificial-intelligence-in-action-in-latin-america-s-schools-evidence-from-peru)
- uDocz was founded in 2018 and offers an academic tutor plus B2B products for institutions and governments — [Descubre.vc](https://www.descubre.vc/udocz) (snippet)
- The page does not give the channel (WhatsApp or web) or the districts/UGELs.

### Inferences
- Taken or crowded: AI math tutoring for 5th-year secondary in Lima public schools (World Bank/uDocz, which also holds DRELM access), adaptive secondary math in the north (Numeral), and content-format personalization in Saco Oliveros (Entiendo.ai).
- Open space: a tool for teachers that makes students' reasoning visible and assesses it (formative assessment of the process, not answer tutoring) through WhatsApp. It could complement tutors instead of competing with them, which suits a positioning around competency-based evaluation (CNEB, the national curriculum) or teacher workload.
- Entiendo.ai's foothold in Saco Oliveros suggests chain schools will consider AI pilots, but that chain is partly occupied.

### Gaps
- Not researched within the budget: Lidera, Innova Schools' own AI and tech stack, and other Peruvian K-12 edtechs.
- Entiendo.ai's pricing, ages and grant amount (fetch blocked).
- Which channel the uDocz tutor uses, and which districts it runs in.

## 2. Market segments: private, low-cost, chains, public JEC, COAR, academies

### Takeaway
Private education serves about a third of students and mostly lower-middle classes (C/D/E). About 60-64% of private enrollment sits in low-cost schools (around S/150-200 a month). Mid-tier chains charge S/500-700 a month. SJL is Lima's largest school district by count, and Lima Norte and Sur have a 4:1 private-to-public school ratio. Current figures for JEC (full-day public secondary schools) and their connectivity were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Private education is 32% of the country's students, and 73% of private-school students are from socioeconomic levels C, D and E. About 3.5M learners attend private institutions across all levels, including higher education — [La Cámara (CCL)](https://lacamara.pe/educacion-privada-crece-mientras-la-publica-se-estanca/)
- Chains: Innova Schools has 63 campuses and about 50,000 students in 17 Peruvian cities. Saco Oliveros has 51 campuses (37 owned, 14 franchised) and about 36,000 students. Futura Schools has 10 campuses and about 4,000 students (Arequipa, Tacna, Ica, Trujillo, Chiclayo, Piura). Fees at Innova, Futura, Saco Oliveros and similar schools run S/500-700 a month — [La Cámara](https://lacamara.pe/educacion-privada-crece-mientras-la-publica-se-estanca/) (article undated, probably 2023)
- Saco Oliveros projects 37,000 students in 2026 with 51 schools — [Gestión tag page](https://gestion.pe/noticias/Saco-Oliveros/) (snippet, Jan 2026)
- Trilce: 18 Lima schools, 6 in the provinces, 7 academies and 24,000+ students — [Computrabajo job posting](https://pe.computrabajo.com/ofertas-de-trabajo/oferta-de-trabajo-de-docente-de-primaria-santa-beatriz-en-lima-0D7DC12713506C4661373E686DCF3405) (snippet; low-quality source)
- Pamer's academy programs prepare students for admission to San Marcos, PUCP, UNI and UNT, with weekly mock admission exams — [El Popular, 2022](https://elpopular.pe/educacion/2022/09/30/5-mejores-academias-postular-universidad-154784) (dated)
- Low-cost schools (under S/200 a month) captured 64% of private enrollment (2018 data) — [Minedu, Tipología y caracterización de escuelas privadas](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12799/6766/Tipolog%C3%ADa%20y%20caracterizaci%C3%B3n%20de%20escuelas%20privadas%20en%20el%20Per%C3%BA.pdf) (snippet)
- The Education Minister said about 60% of private schools charge around S/150 a month on average, 30% around S/300, and nearly 90% under S/500 — [Panamericana / search summary](https://panamericana.pe/locales/404957-colegios-caros-lima) (snippet; interview date unclear)
- Low-cost schools are 61.7% of primary schools in Lima (Minedu 2018, cited in a PUCP thesis) — [PUCP thesis](https://tesis.pucp.edu.pe/repositorio/handle/20.500.12404/25880) (snippet)
- Lima holds 53% of the country's private basic-education enrollment (year unclear) — [Revista SIEP](https://revistas.siep.org.pe/index.php/RPIE/article/download/34/66) (snippet)
- Lima public enrollment in 2025 was 1,219,682 (Censo Educativo 2025) — [ESCALE Magnitudes Lima Metropolitana](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Publicaciones/Boletin%20regional/Magnitudes-LIMA_METROPOLITANA.pdf) (snippet)
- Lima has 6,000+ private schools and about 2,000 public ones. SJL has the most schools (804) and about 165,000 students. In Comas, VMT, VES and Chorrillos, private schools outnumber public ones 4 to 1 — [RPP / Mapcity](https://rpp.pe/lima/actualidad/mapcity-lima-tiene-mas-de-6-mil-colegios-privados-y-casi-2-mil-centros-publicos-noticia-708989) (older article, date not confirmed)
- Private-school fees rose 7-10% in 2023 — [Gestión, pensiones escolares](https://gestion.pe/noticias/pensiones-escolares/) (snippet)
- JEC: created in 2014 (RM 451-2014-MINEDU) and governed by RVM 326-2019-MINEDU. RM 575-2025-MINEDU (23 Dec 2025) removed some schools from the model — [El Peruano](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2471796-1) (snippet). The model includes laptops, internet and 45 hours a week — [GRADE FORGE](https://www.grade.org.pe/wp-content/uploads/FORGE_Evaluación-del-diseño-y-proceso-de-implementación-del-modelo-de-Jornada-Escolar-Completo-JEC-para-educación-secundaria-a-nivel-nacional.pdf). The count of about 2,001 JEC schools is UNVERIFIED (Perú Educa, cited by [Dialnet](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8528317.pdf)).

### Inferences
- Low-cost private schools in Lima Norte/Este/Sur (SJL, Comas, VMT, VES) are the largest pool by volume, but at about S/150 a month they have very thin margins for edtech. A price per student per month of S/2-5 (my estimate, unvalidated) would be needed, or the parent pays.
- Mid-tier chains (S/500-700) have more budget and centralized decision-making: one deal covers 36k-50k students. Entiendo.ai's presence in Saco Oliveros shows the sales path exists. Trilce and Pamer pair schools with preuniversity academies, which links to admission exams heavy on reasoning.
- Academies and pre-university programs: willingness to pay for exam performance is high, but "showing thinking" must be framed as raising admission scores.

- COAR numbers (25 schools is general knowledge, not verified here).

## 3. Regional data: device access and learning outcomes

### Takeaway
Phones are almost universal (about 95-96% of households), but home internet reaches only about 60% of households. Rural areas lag Lima by about 12 points on any ICT. Among 6-17-year-olds, 76-79% used the internet in 2025, mostly by cellphone. Learning results: the south (Tacna, Moquegua, Arequipa) leads, Amazon regions lag, and 6th-grade math is flat. ENLA 2024 did not assess secondary.

### Cited Findings
- Households with at least one ICT, Q1 2025: 95.6% nationally, Lima Metropolitana 98.7%, other urban 97.4%, rural 86.8% — [INEI TIC Q1 2025](https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/boletines/informe-tecnico_tecnologiasdelainformacion_ene_feb_mar2025.pdf) (snippet)
- Q4 2025: mobile phones in 95.7% of households, internet in 60.1%, computers in 36.8% — [INEI boletín TIC oct-dic 2025](https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/boletines/boletin-tic-oct_dic2025.pdf) (snippet; PDF not machine-readable on fetch)
- Ages 6-17 using the internet: 76.0% in Q2 2025 and 79.4% in Q3 2025 — [INEI Niñez IIT2025](https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/boletines/boletin_ninez_iit2025.pdf); [INEI Niñez IIIT2025](https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/boletines/estado-de-la-ninez-y-adolescencia-iii-trimestre-2025.pdf) (snippets)
- Of 6-17-year-olds, 86.4% accessed the internet by cellphone in Q1 2024 — [INEI press note 085-2024](https://m.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/noticias/nota-de-prensa-n-085-2024-inei.docx.pdf). A separate 89.2% figure is in [INEI gob.pe news](https://www.gob.pe/en/institucion/inei/noticias/1242951-inei-el-89-2-de-la-poblacion-de-6-a-17-anos-accedio-al-internet-a-traves-del-celular) (period not confirmed)
- ENLA 2024 assessed 4th grade primary (census) and 6th grade primary (sample) and did not evaluate secondary competencies — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-tacna-moquegua-y-arequipa-obtienen-los-mejores-resultados-evaluacion-aprendizajes-1025686.aspx); [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/267874-evaluacion-de-aprendizaje-2024-escolares-de-primaria-registran-avances-en-matematicas-y-lectura)
- Tacna, Moquegua and Arequipa had the top ENLA 2024 results. Loreto and Ucayali had the largest gains, and Lambayeque led gains in math — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-tacna-moquegua-y-arequipa-obtienen-los-mejores-resultados-evaluacion-aprendizajes-1025686.aspx)
- Only about 3 in 10 4th graders reached a satisfactory level in reading or math, and the gap is worse in the Amazon regions — [La República, Apr 2025](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2025/04/14/minedu-revela-que-solo-3-de-cada-10-escolares-de-cuarto-de-primaria-entiende-lo-que-lee-brecha-se-intensifica-en-regiones-amazonicas-746998)
- 6th-grade math has been flat since 2022 (average 509), and private schools declined — (snippet via search summary; primary source not isolated)
- Only 13% of secondary students reach the expected level in math — [World Bank, Jul 2026](https://www.bancomundial.org/es/results/2026/07/02/artificial-intelligence-in-action-in-latin-america-s-schools-evidence-from-peru)

### Inferences
- WhatsApp works as a channel almost everywhere urban, since phone penetration is near-universal. But the student often uses a shared or parent's phone with prepaid data, so asynchronous, low-data flows (photo of written work, voice notes) suit it better than live sessions.
- Where need meets feasibility: urban areas outside the top performers (Lima Norte/Este, Piura, La Libertad) have high phone access and mediocre results. Arequipa has better results and an active ecosystem (Code en mi cole) but less "pain" to show. Puno and Cusco rural areas have more need but weaker connectivity and harder logistics for a Monday pilot.
- For ages: 6th-grade primary math stagnation and 13% secondary math proficiency both point to upper primary through lower secondary (roughly ages 10-14) as high-pain and less crowded than 5th-year secondary.

- ECE/ENLA secondary results by region (2023 2nd-year secondary, if available).

## 4. Who pays and procurement

### Takeaway
Three proven payer paths: private chains (B2B; Entiendo.ai/Saco Oliveros), multilaterals working with DRELM (World Bank/uDocz, with Big Tech donating AI tokens), and ProInnóvate StartUp Perú non-refundable seed grants (Numeral, Bilingües, Code en mi cole, Entiendo.ai all used them). Selling directly to UGELs, DREs or regional governments was not evidenced in this research.

### Cited Findings
- ProInnóvate/StartUp Perú gave non-refundable seed funds to at least three edtechs in 2026 — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/tres-edtech-peruanas-financiadas-por-proinnovate-en-2026/); Entiendo.ai was co-financed by ProInnóvate — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=832344) (snippet)
- The World Bank model: an edtech (uDocz) builds and deploys, the AI provider (Anthropic) donates tokens, DRELM gives school access, and a university (UPC) trains teachers — [Descubre.vc](https://www.descubre.vc/noticia/udocz-y-el-banco-mundial-lanzan-tutores-de-ia-para-estudiantes-en-per-2026-04-18) (snippet); [World Bank](https://www.bancomundial.org/es/results/2026/07/02/artificial-intelligence-in-action-in-latin-america-s-schools-evidence-from-peru)
- Low-cost private schools work on fees around S/150 a month, which limits the budget for extras — [Minedu typology](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12799/6766/Tipolog%C3%ADa%20y%20caracterizaci%C3%B3n%20de%20escuelas%20privadas%20en%20el%20Per%C3%BA.pdf) (snippet)
- An IDB project approved in June 2025 targets technical skills in JEC schools, so there is multilateral money flowing to JEC — [IDB PE-T1620](https://www.iadb.org/es/proyecto/PE-T1620) (snippet)

### Inferences
- In the short term, free pilots in individual low-cost private schools (the director decides alone, no procurement) are the fastest way to gather evidence. Monetize later through chains or a grant.
- Public schools: getting through UGEL/DRE takes time and needs an ally (a university, a multilateral or an NGO). Use them as a second phase, framed as teacher support aligned with the national curriculum.

### Gaps
- Willingness-to-pay data for edtech in Peruvian private schools (no survey found).
- Size of StartUp Perú grants in 2026 (not retrieved).
- Concytec or foundation calls for proposals.
- Whether municipalities can buy software, and the Perú Compras rules for that.

## 5. Recommendation inputs (synthesis; my inferences, not sourced facts)

### Takeaway
The best combined starting point appears to be low-to-mid-cost private schools in Lima Norte/Este (SJL, Comas, Los Olivos, SMP). Target 5th-6th grade primary through 2nd year of secondary (ages 10-14) in math and reading comprehension, sold to teachers and directors as making student reasoning visible for competency-based evaluation. Phase 2 is a mid-tier chain (Trilce or a similar chain in Lima; Futura/Bryce in Arequipa/Trujillo). Phase 3 is public schools through a multilateral or university partner.

### Cited Findings
- Volume: SJL has 804 schools and about 165,000 students, and in Comas/VMT/VES private schools outnumber public 4 to 1 — [RPP](https://rpp.pe/lima/actualidad/mapcity-lima-tiene-mas-de-6-mil-colegios-privados-y-casi-2-mil-centros-publicos-noticia-708989)
- Pain: 6th-grade math is flat and private schools declined; secondary math proficiency is 13% — [World Bank](https://www.bancomundial.org/es/results/2026/07/02/artificial-intelligence-in-action-in-latin-america-s-schools-evidence-from-peru); [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/267874-evaluacion-de-aprendizaje-2024-escolares-de-primaria-registran-avances-en-matematicas-y-lectura)
- Access: 98.7% of Lima households have an ICT, and most children reach the internet by cellphone — [INEI](https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/boletines/informe-tecnico_tecnologiasdelainformacion_ene_feb_mar2025.pdf); [INEI 085-2024](https://m.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/noticias/nota-de-prensa-n-085-2024-inei.docx.pdf)

### Inferences
- Avoid 5th-year secondary in Lima public schools (the World Bank/uDocz RCT is there; also contamination risk for their trial) and avoid head-on "adaptive math" (Numeral).
- Reachable on the Monday after the hackathon: low-cost private school directors in Lima Norte/Este (no procurement, decide in days). Academies (Pamer/Trilce) are an alternative with a clear "admission exam reasoning" pitch.
- Whether a parent can pay directly (B2C2B) at about S/150-fee schools is untested and should be validated in the pilot.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence on teachers' WhatsApp usage for school work, or on schools' policies on student phones.
- No data on teachers' pain around assessing reasoning (would need interviews).
