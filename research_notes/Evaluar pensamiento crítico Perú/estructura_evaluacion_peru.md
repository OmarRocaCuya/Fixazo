## 1. Estructura: EBR, ciclos, modalidades, tipos de escuela, matrícula

### Takeaway
Educación Básica has three modalities: EBR, EBA and EBE. EBR runs across seven ciclos (I–VII): Inicial (ciclos I–II), Primaria (6 grades, ciclos III–V) and Secundaria (5 grades, ciclos VI–VII). Census 2025 counts about 9.34 M students, 72% in public schools. One national curriculum (CNEB) covers all three modalities.

### Cited Findings
- The CNEB (RM 281-2016-MINEDU) is still the curriculum in force. It is a single curriculum for Educación Básica Regular, Especial and Alternativa, built on competencies, a perfil de egreso, formative assessment and national learning standards. It allows regional and local diversification. — [Minedu, Currículo Nacional](https://www.minedu.gob.pe/curriculo/actualizacion.php)
- Background: an articulated curriculum since 2005 (initial, primary and secondary unified), then the DCN 2009 (competency approach), then the CNEB built over 2012–2016 (14 studies, 33 international curricula reviewed, regional consultations). — [Minedu, Currículo Nacional](https://www.minedu.gob.pe/curriculo/actualizacion.php)
- Ciclos and ages **[CNEB text, not re-fetched]**: I = 0–2 años; II = 3–5 años (Inicial); III = 1.°–2.° primaria; IV = 3.°–4.°; V = 5.°–6.°; VI = 1.°–2.° secundaria; VII = 3.°–5.° secundaria. Competencies have an expected level per ciclo. — [CNEB PDF](https://www.minedu.gob.pe/curriculo/pdf/curriculo-nacional-de-la-educacion-basica.pdf); the expected levels per ciclo are also described in [Minedu orientaciones DPCC](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12799/8795/Orientaciones%20para%20el%20desarrollo%20y%20la%20evaluaci%C3%B3n%20de%20las%20competencias.%20%C3%81rea%20de%20Desarrollo%20Personal%2c%20Ciudadan%C3%ADa%20y%20C%C3%ADvica.pdf?sequence=1)
- Matrícula, Censo Educativo 2025 (SITEAL, citing ESCALE):
  - Total EBR + EBA + EBE: **9,341,956** students, 112,724 servicios educativos, 612,924 docentes.
  - Public: **72%** (6,731,402). Private: **28%** (2,610,554).
  - EBR: 8,293,767, of which Inicial 1,629,014, Primaria 3,666,690 and Secundaria 2,998,063.
  - Source: [SITEAL/UNESCO perfil Perú](https://siteal.iiep.unesco.org/pais/peru). Cross-check: [ESCALE Censo 2022 PPT](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/10156/9345030/PPT_Censo_Educativo_2023_final.pdf) reported 9,412,823 for the earlier census. ESCALE portal: [escale.minedu.gob.pe](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/)
- Low-cost private schools score about the same as urban public schools on ENLA 2024 (4.° primaria). — [La República, 14-04-2025](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2025/04/14/minedu-revela-que-solo-3-de-cada-10-escolares-de-cuarto-de-primaria-entiende-lo-que-lee-brecha-se-intensifica-en-regiones-amazonicas-746998)
- Private-school enrollment trends (matrícula and pensions growing more slowly) — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/economia/matricula-en-colegios-particulares-educacion-colegios-privados-suben-matricula-y-pensiones-a-menor-ritmo-las-razones-detras-noticia/)
- Each school year runs under a Norma Técnica for public and private schools. The 2026 edition exists (fetch blocked). — [Norma Técnica Año Escolar 2026](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12799/11753/Norma%20T%C3%A9cnica%20para%20el%20A%C3%B1o%20Escolar%202026%20en%20las%20Instituciones%20y%20Programas%20Educativos%20P%C3%BAblicos%20y%20Privados%20de%20la%20Educaci%C3%B3n%20B%C3%A1sica.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y)

### Inferences
- A tool built for EBR secundaria public schools reaches about 3 M secondary students. Most of them use the same CNEB competencies and the AD–C scale, so one competency taxonomy covers almost the whole market.
- EBA and EBE also use the CNEB, so the same competency mapping could carry over to them, with adaptations.

## 2. CNEB 2016: competencias, perfil, enfoques; dónde está el pensamiento crítico

### Takeaway
The CNEB defines 31 competencias, an 11-point perfil de egreso, 7 enfoques transversales, and for each competency: capacidades, estándares by ciclo, and desempeños by grade or age. Critical thinking is not a stand-alone competency. It sits inside specific capacidades, mainly in reading, inquiry, history, citizenship deliberation and math argumentation, plus the transversal "Gestiona su aprendizaje".

### Cited Findings
- A competency is the ability to combine capacidades to reach a purpose in a given situation, acting pertinently and ethically. Competencies develop along levels expected per ciclo. — [Minedu orientaciones DPCC](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12799/8795/Orientaciones%20para%20el%20desarrollo%20y%20la%20evaluaci%C3%B3n%20de%20las%20competencias.%20%C3%81rea%20de%20Desarrollo%20Personal%2c%20Ciudadan%C3%ADa%20y%20C%C3%ADvica.pdf?sequence=1)
- Secondary covers 31 competencies across 11 áreas curriculares. — [Raiko/SciELO article](https://ve.scielo.org/pdf/raiko/v8s1/2542-3088-raiko-8-s1-529.pdf). Another secondary source cites 28, which probably counts without the 2 transversal competencies or without some area. This conflicts, so verify in the CNEB.
- **[CNEB text, not re-fetched]** [CNEB PDF](https://www.minedu.gob.pe/curriculo/pdf/curriculo-nacional-de-la-educacion-basica.pdf):
  - 31 competencias = 29 area competencies + 2 transversal ones: "Se desenvuelve en entornos virtuales generados por las TIC" and "Gestiona su aprendizaje de manera autónoma".
  - Perfil de egreso: 11 aprendizajes.
  - 7 enfoques transversales: de derechos; inclusivo o de atención a la diversidad; intercultural; igualdad de género; ambiental; orientación al bien común; búsqueda de la excelencia.
  - Estándares de aprendizaje describe progression by ciclo. Desempeños describe observable actions by grade or age.
- Competencies and capacidades where critical thinking lives **[CNEB text, not re-fetched]**:
  - **Lee diversos tipos de textos escritos en su lengua materna** (Comunicación):
    - obtiene información del texto escrito
    - infiere e interpreta información del texto
    - **reflexiona y evalúa la forma, el contenido y contexto del texto**
    - Minedu's own training material confirms the wording "reflexiona y evalúa la forma, el contenido y contexto del texto escrito": [Minedu fascículo lectura y escritura](https://formacionenservicio.minedu.gob.pe/sifods/centro-recurso/2022/Recursos-de-Acciones-formativas/Mejora-de-Aprendizajes/Cursos-Primeros-anios-de-la-escolaridad/Curso-La-lectura-y-escritura-en-los-primeros-anios-de-la-escolaridad/Fasciculo-Unidad-1-Sesion-3-La-lectura-y-la-escritura-en-los-primeros-a%c3%b1os-de-la-escolaridad.pdf.pdf)
  - **Indaga mediante métodos científicos para construir sus conocimientos** (Ciencia y Tecnología): problematiza situaciones; diseña estrategias para hacer indagación; genera y registra datos o información; analiza datos e información; evalúa y comunica el proceso y resultados.
  - **Construye interpretaciones históricas** (Ciencias Sociales): interpreta críticamente fuentes diversas; comprende el tiempo histórico; elabora explicaciones sobre procesos históricos.
  - **Convive y participa democráticamente en la búsqueda del bien común** (DPCC / Personal Social): includes the capacidad "delibera sobre asuntos públicos", the most explicit argumentation capacity.
  - **Gestiona su aprendizaje de manera autónoma** (transversal): define metas de aprendizaje; organiza acciones estratégicas; monitorea y ajusta su desempeño. This is the metacognitive layer.
  - Also relevant: the math competencies' capacidad "argumenta afirmaciones…", and "Explica el mundo físico… / evalúa implicancias del saber científico". Wording should be verified.

### Inferences
- A tool that reports results against these named capacidades, using the CNEB's exact wording, would map straight onto the criteria teachers already write. It would not add a new framework on top of the teacher's work.
- "Reflexiona y evalúa" in reading is the best single anchor. It is the capacity the national tests (ENLA) report as the hardest, so it carries both curricular and accountability weight.

### Gaps
- The full CNEB text could not be re-fetched (PDF over 10 MB). Verbatim capacity names should be checked against the CNEB or the Programas Curriculares de Primaria and Secundaria before use in product copy.
- Not verified: the exact desempeños by grade for "reflexiona y evalúa".

## 3. Reglas de evaluación: RVM 094-2020-MINEDU, escala AD–C, actualizaciones 2023–2026

### Takeaway
Classroom assessment is governed by RVM N.° 094-2020-MINEDU (Norma que regula la evaluación de las competencias de los estudiantes de la Educación Básica). It uses a formative, competency-based approach and the literal scale AD/A/B/C. Teachers analyze evidence against criterios, make a period cut with a nivel de logro and a conclusión descriptiva, and record it in SIAGIE. As of mid-2026 the scale is still AD–C. I found no 2026 norm restoring the 0–20 vigesimal scale.

### Cited Findings
- The governing norm is RVM 00094-2020-MINEDU. The literal scale AD, A, B, C is set in the CNEB and communicates niveles de logro. — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/peru/colegios-calificaran-con-ad-a-alumnos-destacados-y-con-c-al-que-muestre-progreso-minimo-noticia/); official text at gob.pe normas legales 541161-094-2020-minedu (fetch returned HTTP 418): [gob.pe](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minedu/normas-legales/541161-094-2020-minedu)
- Scale meanings: AD = logro destacado, A = logro esperado, B = en proceso, C = en inicio (minimum progress). — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-colegios-calificaran-ad-a-alumnos-destacados-y-c-al-muestre-un-progreso-minimo-966438.aspx); [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/peru/colegios-calificaran-con-ad-a-alumnos-destacados-y-con-c-al-que-muestre-progreso-minimo-noticia/)
- Results are reported to the student, family and system each bimestre, trimestre or semestre, to identify progress, barriers and needs. — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-colegios-calificaran-ad-a-alumnos-destacados-y-c-al-muestre-un-progreso-minimo-966438.aspx)
- At the end of each period the teacher makes a "corte" to report the nivel de logro from analysis of the available evidence. Conclusiones descriptivas describe progress and difficulties. They are not isolated grades, averages, loose phrases or adjectives. — [Minedu Formación en Servicio, caja de herramientas EBA](https://formacionenservicio.minedu.gob.pe/sifods/centro-recurso/2022/Recursos-de-Acciones-formativas/Mejora-de-Aprendizajes/Cursos-DEBA/Curso-Planificacion-de-las-experiencias-de-aprendizaje-en-EBA/Caja-de-herramientas/Caja-de-herramientas-U3-S2-Planificacion-de-EdA-EBA.pdf)
- At year end, each student is promovido, goes to recuperación pedagógica, or permanece en el grado, based on the niveles de logro reached in each competency. — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-colegios-calificaran-ad-a-alumnos-destacados-y-c-al-muestre-un-progreso-minimo-966438.aspx)
- 2026 status: AD–C is still the scale for the 2026 school year. — [América TV, 2026](https://www.americatv.com.pe/noticias/util-e-interesante/que-significan-notas-peru-y-como-se-repite-ano-escolar-n500164); [Infobae, 08-05-2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/05/08/calificaciones-ad-a-b-y-c-en-el-ano-escolar-2026-que-significan-y-cual-se-necesita-para-aprobar-los-examenes-segun-minedu/)
- Infobae (search snippet only; the full fetch failed) reports secondary promotion rules: 1.°, 3.° and 4.° need at least "B" in half or more of the competencies; 2.° and 5.° need "A" or "AD" in at least half. **Unverified.** It conflicts with the general "promoción" logic of the RVM and should be checked against RVM 094-2020 and its amendments. — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/05/08/calificaciones-ad-a-b-y-c-en-el-ano-escolar-2026-que-significan-y-cual-se-necesita-para-aprobar-los-examenes-segun-minedu/)
- History of the scale:
  - 2019: Minedu backtracked and kept AD–C only for 1.° de secundaria, with vigesimal for 2.°–5.°. — [Educación en Red, 2019](https://noticia.educacionenred.pe/2019/02/minedu-descarta-nuevo-sistema-calificacion-letras-ano-seran-escala-ad-a-b-c-168928.html)
  - 2020: proposal to extend letters to all of secundaria. — [La República, 2020](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2020/02/11/minedu-deesde-este-ano-usarian-calificaciones-con-letras-para-todo-secundaria)
  - Secondary was later fully moved to the literal scale. — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-minedu-asi-sera-calificacion-competencias-secundaria-740989.aspx)
  - For rankings, numeric equivalences apply: AD=4, A=3, B=2, C=1. — [Educación en Red](https://noticia.educacionenred.pe/2019/02/minedu-descarta-nuevo-sistema-calificacion-letras-ano-seran-escala-ad-a-b-c-168928.html)
  - Debate on the change: [PUCP PuntoEdu](https://puntoedu.pucp.edu.pe/archivo/detras-del-cambio-de-escala-de-notas/); [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/peru/expertos-cuestionan-nuevo-sistema-calificacion-escolar-noticia-605186-noticia/)
- The 2020 proposal mentioned that teachers should share rubrics with students before assessments. This is a pre-final source and may not appear in the final norm. — [La República 2020](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2020/02/11/minedu-deesde-este-ano-usarian-calificaciones-con-letras-para-todo-secundaria)

### Inferences
- The teacher's real workflow unit is: criterios de evaluación (derived from the competency's desempeños) → evidencias (student products and actions) → retroalimentación → period nivel de logro AD–C per competency → conclusión descriptiva (for B/C, or as required) → SIAGIE entry.
- A tool that outputs an evidence-backed suggested level (AD/A/B/C) per capacity, plus a draft conclusión descriptiva, slots into the "corte" step. That is where workload peaks.
- Since grading is per competency rather than per test, a single critical-thinking task can serve as evidence for several competencies at once, for example "Lee…" and "Convive… (delibera)".

### Gaps
- Could not read the official RVM 094-2020 text (gob.pe blocked). Not verified: exact articles, approval date, the rules on when conclusiones descriptivas are mandatory, and how competencias transversales are reported.
- Not found: the amendments to RVM 094-2020 from 2021–2026, and whether the 2026 Norma Técnica changed period cuts or SIAGIE rules.
- No 2026 proposal to return to numeric grades was found in these searches. That is absence of evidence, not proof.

## 4. Cómo lo hacen los docentes realmente; evaluaciones nacionales UMC (ENLA)

### Takeaway
Peruvian studies keep finding a gap between the formative-assessment norm and practice. Feedback is often only corrective, and measurement-centered habits persist. Rubrics are seen as helpful for objectivity and time. Nationally, ENLA 2025 shows few students reach Satisfactorio in reading, and the share falls sharply with grade: 32.7% in 4.° primaria, 18.5% in 2.° secundaria, 11.3% in 5.° secundaria. Inferring, integrating and critically evaluating texts are flagged as the weak skills.

### Cited Findings
- Formative assessment appears in official documents and guides but "is not yet a reality" in teaching practice. Studies report lack of knowledge of its processes and resistance, with traditional methods still in use. — [Dialnet study](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8111832.pdf)
- In contexts like Pucallpa, feedback stays merely corrective because of insufficient teacher training and a school culture centered on quantitative measurement (systematic review). — [Revista Horizontes](https://revistahorizontes.org/index.php/revistahorizontes/article/download/2805/3809/12080)
- Rubrics are recognized for improving objectivity, easing constructive feedback, and reducing grading time. — [Dialnet review on rubrics](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8750564.pdf)
- Local intervention studies design programs around the intencional, continua and retroalimentación dimensions of formative assessment (Trujillo). — [Sciendo UNITRU](https://revistas.unitru.edu.pe/index.php/SCIENDO/article/download/5429/5734/21419)
- ENLA 2024: applied to 4.° and 6.° primaria in public and private schools, with 600k+ students. In 4.° primaria lectura, 32.8% reached Satisfactorio nationally; Loreto reached 12.8%. 6.° primaria results were stagnant (math average about 509 since 2022). Private schools in 6.° fell 11 points in reading. — [La República, 14-04-2025](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2025/04/14/minedu-revela-que-solo-3-de-cada-10-escolares-de-cuarto-de-primaria-entiende-lo-que-lee-brecha-se-intensifica-en-regiones-amazonicas-746998); regional results: [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-tacna-moquegua-y-arequipa-obtienen-los-mejores-resultados-evaluacion-aprendizajes-1025686.aspx); [DRE Ayacucho](https://dreayacucho.gob.pe/storage/documentos/2025/notas-de-prensa/01JRBPQRKTPFWJY6YPVBZ2MQMS.pdf)
- ENLA 2025 (results published May 2026), lectura, % Satisfactorio:
  - By grade: 4.° primaria **32.7%**; 2.° secundaria **18.5%**; 5.° secundaria **11.3%**.
  - Gaps in 4.° primaria: private about 41% vs public about 29%; urban about 35.5% vs rural 16.9%; high NSE 54% vs very low NSE 21.2%.
  - Cohort view: the 5.° secundaria cohort of 2025 had 34.8% Satisfactorio in 4.° primaria in 2018. The author warns this is not a direct decline, because test design and cognitive complexity differ.
  - Students struggle to infer, integrate information and critically analyze texts.
  - Source: [De la evidencia al aula blog, 18-05-2026](https://delaevidenciaalaula.wordpress.com/2026/05/18/algunas-reflexiones-sobre-los-ultimos-resultados-enla-2025/)
- ENLA 2025 headline: about 88% of 5.° secundaria students do not reach Satisfactorio in math or reading ("1 de cada 10"). — [Infobae, 20-05-2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/05/20/enla-2025-el-88-de-los-alumnos-de-quinto-de-secundaria-no-alcanza-el-nivel-satisfactorio-en-matematica-ni-en-lectura/); [La República, 18-05-2026](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2026/05/18/educacion-en-alerta-solo-1-de-cada-10-estudiantes-termina-el-colegio-con-nivel-esperado-en-matematica-y-lectura-1262322); [Expreso](https://www.expreso.com.pe/actualidad/enla-revelo-que-solo-1-de-cada-10-alumnos-de-5-grado-de-secundaria-logra-nivel-esperado-casi-el-90-no-alcanza-aprendizajes-satisfactorios-en-matematica-y-lectura-noticia/1286924/)
- Official UMC sources (unreachable from this environment): [ENLA 2025 Resumen ejecutivo](http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Resumen-ejecutivo-ENLA-2025.pdf); [Resultados ENLA 2025 portal](http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/resultadosenla2025/)
- 2.° secundaria (search snippet citing Minedu): results stable vs 2023 in reading and math; "En inicio" and "Previo al inicio" in reading fell by 2.0 percentage points. Loreto secundaria reading: 4.2% Satisfactorio. — [UMC Loreto regional PPT](http://umc.minedu.gob.pe/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/PPTRegional_ENLA2025_Loreto.pdf) (via search, not fetched)
- Achievement levels used by ENLA: Satisfactorio, En proceso, En inicio, Previo al inicio. — [La República 2025](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2025/04/14/minedu-revela-que-solo-3-de-cada-10-escolares-de-cuarto-de-primaria-entiende-lo-que-lee-brecha-se-intensifica-en-regiones-amazonicas-746998)
- The UMC also publishes a PISA 2018 reading framework in Spanish, useful as a conceptual reference for critical reading processes. — [UMC PISA 2018 marco lectura](https://umc.minedu.gob.pe/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Marco-de-evaluacio%CC%81n-de-la-competencia-lectora-de-PISA-2018.pdf)

### Inferences
- The national data justify the need: less than 1 in 5 lower-secondary students, and about 1 in 9 upper-secondary students, reads at the expected level. The deficit is concentrated in inferential and critical-reflective processing.
- Teachers are formally required to give formative feedback, but in practice they lack time and training. A tool that gives rubric-based, capacity-aligned feedback fills the documented gap without adding new requirements.
- ENLA levels (Satisfactorio…Previo al inicio) are a different scale from classroom AD–C. A tool should keep classroom AD–C as its primary output and treat ENLA alignment as secondary context.

### Gaps
- Not found in time: ENLA results broken down by reading process (literal / inferential / critical-reflective). The blog explicitly says it does not report them. The UMC "informes para docentes" or "marco de fundamentación" would have them.
- ENLA 2025 math percentages by grade were not obtained.
- No representative survey of Peruvian teachers on assessment workload, rubric use or SIAGIE burden was found. The evidence is small qualitative or local studies and systematic reviews. GRADE, CNE or IPE reports were not searched for lack of budget.

## 5. Actualización curricular 2026 (RM 393-2026-MINEDU)

### Takeaway
RM 393-2026-MINEDU (reported 19 July 2026) creates a temporary Comisión Sectorial and four macro-regional working groups to supervise and evaluate the update of the CNEB. Both run until 31 December 2027. No new curriculum has been approved yet, so the CNEB 2016 and its assessment rules stay in force through at least 2027.

### Cited Findings
- RM N.° 393-2026-MINEDU sets up the Comisión Sectorial:
  - Chair: the Viceministro de Gestión Pedagógica.
  - Members: Minedu directorates, the Secretaría de Planificación Estratégica and the Consejo Nacional de Educación.
  - Functions: monitor the update, check coherence with policies and regional needs, and issue technical reports.
  - Four Grupos de Trabajo Sectoriales (Macro Norte, Sur, Centro, Oriente) consolidate input from regional curriculum-evaluation tables. These include regional governments, universities, teacher-training institutes and unions.
  - Members serve ad honorem. Both bodies run until 31-12-2027. The article gives no content changes.
  - Source: [El Peruano, 19-07-2026](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/300608-minedu-crea-comision-para-supervisar-la-actualizacion-del-curriculo-nacional-conoce-sus-funciones); also [Diario Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/peru/crean-comision-para-actualizar-el-curriculo-nacional-minedu-oficializa-el-proceso-modificador-noticia/)
- The update builds on a participatory curriculum evaluation run in 2022–2024 (search-result summary of the El Peruano and Correo coverage). — [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/300608-minedu-crea-comision-para-supervisar-la-actualizacion-del-curriculo-nacional-conoce-sus-funciones)
- Related: RVM 085-2026-MINEDU (May 2026) annex says DIGEBR proposed changes to add scientific, biological and ethical content in the update and to remove references to educación sexual integral. Only a fragment was seen. — [LP Derecho, Anexo RVM 085-2026](https://img.lpderecho.pe/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Anexo-Resolucion-Viceministerial-085-2026-Minedu-LPDerecho.pdf)
