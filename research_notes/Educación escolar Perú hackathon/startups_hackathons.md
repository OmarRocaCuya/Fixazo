# EdTech startups (Peru/LatAm/global) and education hackathon winners — landscape for Hack the Andes (Educación Escolar)

Research date: 2026-10-17. About 18 search/fetch calls. Several sources are search-result summaries or secondary aggregators; caveats are noted inline. Many named companies in the brief (Mentu, Lidera, Qinaya, Geekie, Árvore, Lirmi, Puntaje Nacional, Ludik, M-Shule, Eneza, Ustad Mobile, Panorama, BrightBytes, SIMPADE, Uruguay) could not be verified within the call budget. They are listed under Gaps and must not be described as verified.

## 1. Peruvian and LatAm EdTech for K-12 public schools, teachers and families

### Takeaway
Peru's school-level edtech scene is small, early-stage and kept alive by grants. Recent ventures (Numeral, Bilingües, Code en mi cole, Entiendo.ai) depend on non-reimbursable ProInnóvate/StartUp Perú money and pilot in private schools. Across the region, only about 10% of edtech investment goes to K-12. Brazil's Letrus is the clearest example of a B2G model that scaled, through state-network contracts, but independent causal evidence for it is thin.

### Cited Findings
- On 8 Sep 2026, PRODUCE (ProInnóvate / StartUp Perú) presented three funded edtechs, all with non-reimbursable funds and no amounts disclosed. (1) **Numeral** (La Libertad): a secondary-school math platform with personalized paths. (2) **Bilingües**: a 100% virtual English school built on games and rewards. (3) **Code en mi cole** (Arequipa): students learn programming by building video games, and the startup also trains teachers in computational thinking, AI and coding. — [Ecosistema Startup, 11 Sep 2026](https://ecosistemastartup.com/tres-edtech-peruanas-financiadas-por-proinnovate-en-2026/)
- Code en mi cole sells to both students and teachers, which the publisher's own analysis says reduces typical B2B edtech friction. That view is the publisher's, not PRODUCE's. — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/tres-edtech-peruanas-financiadas-por-proinnovate-en-2026/)
- **Entiendo.ai** (Peruvian AI edtech) received ProInnóvate co-funding through the PIMEN line and joined Venture Ready. It is being piloted at the private school chain Saco Oliveros, which measures content adaptation and the teacher and student experience before scaling. — [Ecosistema Startup – Entiendo.ai](https://ecosistemastartup.com/entiendo-ai-edtech-peruana-con-ia-entra-a-venture-ready/)
- A ProInnóvate call selected about 15 edtechs and gave them technical assistance plus seed capital of S/ 50,000–150,000, as reported via Andina. This is a secondary summary and the date is not confirmed. — [Innovación Digital 360 – potencial edtech peruano](https://www.innovaciondigital360.com/industria-4-0/cual-es-el-potencial-edtech-peruano)
- StartUPC (UPC's accelerator) ran a 2025-I call for 10 edtech startups that offered mentoring and expansion toward Mexico. — [Gestión – StartUPC](https://gestion.pe/peru/potenciamiento-digital-el-aumento-de-las-startups-en-latinoamerica-y-el-plan-de-startupc-para-acelerarlas-noticia/)
- The EdTech Summit 2025 was held in Lima as part of Peru Tech Week. — [Revista Economía](https://www.revistaeconomia.com/?p=107542)
- Seedtable lists 7 funded Peruvian edtechs, led by Kurios, Crack the Code and Talently. Crack the Code focuses on kids' coding; Kurios and Talently target adults and tech talent. This is a commercial directory, so treat it with caution. — [Seedtable – Best EdTech Startups in Peru](https://seedtable.com/best-edtech-startups-in-peru)
- In 2022, Peruvian edtech was consolidating around B2B corporate training, which suggests the corporate market has been easier to reach than public schools. — [Gestión 2022 – consolidación edtech B2B](https://gestion.pe/tecnologia/nuevos-jugadores-en-el-mercado-local-la-consolidacion-de-las-edtechs-en-el-b2b-noticia/)
- According to Pecap, cited by El Peruano, only 12% of education startups manage to consolidate. — [El Peruano – ecosistema edtech](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/136785-avanza-ecosistema-de-tecnologia-educativa-en-el-peru)
- **Letrus (Brazil, AI essay feedback, B2G).** The Espírito Santo state network has used Letrus in 121 schools since 2019 and reports that 90% of students improved their ENEM essay scores. There is no comparison group. Letrus also won the UNESCO King Hamad Bin Isa Al-Khalifa Prize. — [A Gazeta](https://www.agazeta.com.br/anuario/alunos-da-rede-estadual-do-es-aprendem-redacao-com-chatgpt-do-enem-0124)
- J-PAL has a case study of Letrus as AI to strengthen the writing of public high-school seniors preparing for ENEM. The snippets retrieved did not include the impact estimates. — [J-PAL case study](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/case-study/artificial-intelligence-strengthen-high-school-students-writing-skills?lang=es)
- The SESI network reports an 11.3% rise in its average with the tool (ENEM 2021: 664 vs. a national 634). This is self-reported and uncontrolled. — [Portal da Indústria](https://noticias.portaldaindustria.com.br/noticias/educacao/inteligencia-artificial-concilia-conhecimento-humano-e-tecnologico-em-sala-de-aula/)
- An academic critique in Revista da Abralin says there is little research on Letrus and questions whether it works as a literacy program. — [Revista Abralin](https://revista.abralin.org/index.php/abralin/article/download/2233/2816/17977)
- The public–private gap is larger in ENEM essays than in other sections, up to 47% in some states in 2023. That analysis was produced by Letrus itself, which is a conflict of interest. — [Diário do Pará](https://diariodopara.com.br/noticias/brasil/redacao-do-enem-desafia-aluno-da-rede-estadual-e-abismo-com-escola-particular-chega-a-47)

### Inferences
- Peru's realistic first customers are private school chains (Saco Oliveros) and grant programs (ProInnóvate). MINEDU and the UGELs are not. A hackathon product should show a path that does not depend on a national government contract on day one, for example a free teacher-facing tool or a pilot with one UGEL or DRE.
- Recent Peruvian entrants cluster in **subject content** (math, English, coding). Teacher-workload tools, attendance and dropout follow-up with families, and school infrastructure and services look much less crowded among Peruvian startups. This is inferred from what was found, not from an exhaustive census.
- Letrus shows the B2G pattern that worked in Brazil: one narrow, high-stakes pain point (the ENEM essay), sold to a state network, with teacher time saved on grading. The weak causal evidence is a warning: judges and governments will increasingly ask for measurable outcomes.

### Gaps
- Mentu, Lidera, Qinaya, Innova Schools' tech, Laboratoria (adult, not K-12), Crehana (adult): no 2022–2026 sources were retrieved on traction or model.
- Geekie, Árvore (Brazil); Lirmi, Puntaje Nacional (Chile); Ludik (Colombia); Mexican K-12 edtechs: not verified.
- Brazilian WhatsApp literacy tutors ("Alfabetiza"-type): not verified.
- Documented Peruvian edtech failures or shutdowns: none found.

## 2. WhatsApp/SMS/phone-first education in low-connectivity settings

### Takeaway
Low-tech channels have some of the strongest RCT evidence in education. Phone tutoring (Youth Impact ConnectEd) shows about 0.33 SD across several countries, and WhatsApp AI math tutoring (Rori, Ghana) shows about 0.37 SD in one preprint RCT. SMS alone did not work; what worked was interaction plus targeted level.

### Cited Findings
- **Rori (Rising Academies), WhatsApp AI math tutor.** An RCT in Ghana with about 1,000 students in grades 3–9 across 11 schools used two 30-minute sessions a week for 8 months. It found an effect size of 0.37 on math growth (p < 0.001). This is an arXiv preprint, not peer-reviewed. — [arXiv 2402.09809](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09809v1)
- Anthropic's vendor case study reports a 0.3 SD effect, more than 150,000 students reached, and use in Ghana, Sierra Leone, Nigeria, Kenya and Rwanda. This is a vendor source and conflicts slightly with the 0.37 SD figure. — [Claude case study – Rising Academies](https://claude.com/es/customers/rising-academies)
- HundrED's profile says Rori "could more than double" math learning. This is aspirational, not a tested result. — [HundrED – Rori](https://hundred.org/en/innovations/rori)
- Mail & Guardian (2023) covered a WhatsApp maths hotline in South Africa. — [Mail & Guardian](https://mg.co.za/education/2023-09-18-whatsapp-maths-hotline-is-a-winning-formula)
- **Youth Impact ConnectEd (phone + SMS, targeted instruction).** Phone calls combined with SMS raised learning by 0.33 SD on average, over a third above the median education intervention. In Botswana, SMS alone produced no significant gains (Angrist et al., 2022). The model was replicated across India, Kenya, Nepal, the Philippines and Uganda, with Youth Impact citing a 65% increase in the share of students who learned division. — [Youth Impact blog](https://www.youth-impact.org/blog/can-phone-tutoring-improve-learning%3F-new-global-evidence-says-yes); [J-PAL 20 for 20](https://povertyactionlab.org/node/8131252)
- Youth Impact calls it one of the most cost-effective education programs per dollar. That is the organization's own claim. — [Youth Impact – ConnectEd](https://youth-impact.org/copy-of-connected)

### Inferences
- For rural Peru (Andes, Amazonia), the evidence favors **interactive, level-targeted** delivery over broadcast content, which is the "Aprendo en casa" style. A WhatsApp or voice tutor or teacher-assistant that adapts to level is evidence-aligned. A content-push bot is not.
- Spanish/Quechua WhatsApp tutoring tied to Peru's national curriculum (CNEB) did not show up in the searches as an existing product. That is a possible white space but is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- M-Shule, Eneza Education (Kenya; Eneza reportedly struggled), Ustad Mobile: no sources retrieved.
- Any LatAm Spanish WhatsApp tutor with RCT evidence: not found.

## 3. AI teacher copilots (global and LatAm Spanish)

### Takeaway
Teacher copilots are a crowded, well-funded category, and several are now free (MagicSchool freemium, Khanmigo for Teachers free and in Spanish for LatAm since Jan 2025). The best independent trial (Oak's Aila, EEF/NFER) found about 24% less planning time but low actual use (about 7% of lessons). A generic "AI lesson planner" is therefore a clone. Any differentiation has to be local: the CNEB/MINEDU formats, multigrade rural classrooms, low connectivity, or Quechua.

### Cited Findings
- **MagicSchool.** $45M Series B led by Valor Equity Partners (Feb 2025), after a $15M Series A from Bain Capital Ventures (Jun 2024) and $2.4M in 2023. About 4.5M users in early 2025, "eight-figure revenue", more than 10,000 schools, and a presence in 160 countries. — [BusinessDen](https://businessden.com/?p=124059); [MagicSchool blog](https://www.magicschool.ai/blog-posts/series-b-fundraise-for-teacher-ai); [BusinessWire Series A](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240627356064/en/MagicSchool-AI-Raises-$15-Million-to-Fight-Teacher-Burnout-and-Prepare-Students-for-the-AI-Future)
- A later secondary report citing Crunchbase claims about 8M registered educators, about $63M raised in total, and revenue roughly tripling year over year by end-2025. This comes from a single secondary source. — [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3937907925040262)
- **Khanmigo for Teachers.** Free for US K-12 teachers since 21 May 2024 (previously $4/month), funded by Microsoft Azure. — [Microsoft News](https://news.microsoft.com/source/features/ai/khan-academy-and-microsoft-partner-to-expand-access/); [AI for Education](https://www.aiforeducation.io/blog/khanmigo-for-teachers-now-free)
- On 29 Jan 2025, Khanmigo for Teachers became available in Spain and Latin America in Spanish and English, as part of a pilot in dozens of countries. — [Khan Academy blog](https://blog.khanacademy.org/teachers-in-44-countries-now-get-free-access-to-khanmigo-in-english-thanks-to-microsoft-support)
- **Oak National Academy Aila (UK, public).** An EEF-funded RCT evaluated by NFER found that Aila teachers spent 76% as much prep time as the comparison group (about 2.5 vs. 3.3 h/week, roughly 49 min saved), with no drop in resource quality. Of the Aila group, 81% used it at least once, but it was used for only about 7% of lessons, use declined during the term, and teacher opinions were mixed. Sources disagree on sample size (464 teachers in 108 schools vs. 86 schools). — [EEF project page](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/projects/aila-teacher-choices-trial); [EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/oaks-aila-saves-primary-teachers-49-minutes-a-week-on-lesson-planning-eef-trial-finds); [ResultSense, Oct 2026](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-10-06-eef-trial-oak-aila-lesson-planning-time/)
- The UK government publishes an algorithmic transparency record for Aila, a public-sector governance precedent. — [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/algorithmic-transparency-records/oak-national-academy-aila-oaks-ai-lesson-assistant)

### Inferences
- Time saved is real but modest, and habitual use is the hard part. A winning pitch should target a **recurring, mandatory** teacher task in Peru that copilots ignore, for example MINEDU-format "sesiones de aprendizaje", competency-based assessment records (SIAGIE grading), "carpeta pedagógica" paperwork, and multigrade planning. It should not target generic lesson plans.
- Given free Khanmigo in Spanish and MagicSchool's scale, competing head-on is a clone. Integration with the MINEDU workflow and offline/WhatsApp delivery are the defensible angles.

### Gaps
- Brisk Teaching adoption numbers: not retrieved.
- Spanish-native LatAm teacher copilots (startups or ministry tools): not found. Whether MINEDU/PerúEduca has an official AI assistant in 2026: not verified.

## 4. Early-warning / dropout-prevention systems and outcomes

### Takeaway
Peru already has a national ML early-warning system, **Alerta Escuela** (MINEDU, inside SIAGIE, since late 2020). Chile has the SAT (since 2019). Prediction is therefore not white space. The documented weak link is what happens after the flag: action, family contact and case follow-up. The World Bank notes that evidence on EWS effectiveness is limited.

### Cited Findings
- **Alerta Escuela (Peru).** Principals access it through SIAGIE. It classifies each student as green, yellow or orange for risk of interrupting studies, and principals record monthly data such as remote-learning access and contact with teachers. — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-minedu-implementa-sistema-identifica-a-estudiantes-riesgo-abandonar-escuela-817316.aspx)
- MINEDU launched it in late 2020 as a web platform where principals and tutors see each student's dropout risk. It was co-evaluated with the World Bank, and the blog stresses that evidence on EWS effectiveness is limited. — [World Bank blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/education/early-warning-systems-can-help-prevent-dropouts-administrators-need-consider-critical)
- A 2024 MINEDU methodology paper describes an ML model of inter-annual dropout risk for basic education (EBR), trained mostly on administrative data, with satisfactory precision and recall for initial, primary and secondary levels. — [Repositorio MINEDU](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/10990)
- A MINEDU policy brief (Edudatos 48) on early warning for interrupted studies cites that not finishing secondary school can cut the probability of adequate employment by 20% (INEI 2018). — [Edudatos 48](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Analisis/Edudatos/Edudatos%2048%20-%20Policy%20brief%20Alerta%20temprana%20interrupcion%20de%20estudios.pdf)
- A PreventionWeb record lists a related study by Alegría, Avitabile and Chumpitaz Torres on the Peru EWS. — [PreventionWeb](https://www.preventionweb.net/es/node/78191)
- **Chile SAT.** Launched in 2019 by Mineduc and Desarrollo Social, it uses attendance, grade repetition, performance, socioeconomic level and family environment. It was piloted in 2020 and announced for extension to all public schools. — [Mineduc SAT](https://www.mineduc.cl/sistema-de-alerta-temprana-contra-la-desercion-escolar/); [La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/nacional/noticia/funcionara-sistema-alerta-temprana-escolar/826668/)
- In 2021, Chile reported 39,498 children not enrolled, on top of more than 186,000 already out of the system. The highest dropout grade was 1° medio (7,048 students). — [Mineduc 2021](https://www.mineduc.cl/mineduc-entrega-detalles-de-cifras-de-desercion-escolar-2021/)

### Inferences
- A hackathon team should **not** build "an ML model that predicts dropout." Alerta Escuela already does this with MINEDU's own data. The white space is the **last mile**: turning an orange flag into a tracked intervention, such as tutor or family WhatsApp outreach, home-visit logging, attendance capture in schools with poor SIAGIE usage, and closing the loop back to the principal. An add-on that consumes or complements Alerta Escuela is more credible than a replacement.

### Gaps
- Alerta Escuela impact-evaluation numbers: not retrieved (the World Bank mentions the evaluation without figures in snippets).
- Colombia SIMPADE, Uruguay's EWS, Panorama Education and BrightBytes outcomes: not retrieved.
- Chile SAT evaluation 2022–2026: not found.

## 5. Winning education hackathon projects 2022–2026

### Takeaway
MINEDU's own hackathons reward robotics, STEAM and maker/AI projects by students and teachers (2024 teacher winner: "Sessiontec", makerspace learning sessions). Globally, many recent education hackathon entries are offline-first AI tutors, so that idea is common. No public record of previous "Hack the Andes" winners was found.

### Cited Findings
- **MINEDU Hackathon 2025** ("Hackathon en Tecnologías Digitales", STEAM and AI, 4th edition). The basic-education student category went to **NeuroBots** (girls from Callao) for an "autonomous robot for transforming plastics". The budget exceeded S/ 219,000, and female participation rose 28% between 2023 and 2025. — [ProActivo](https://proactivo.com.pe/minedu-premia-el-talento-estudiantil-y-docente-en-hackathon-2025/); [El Peruano](https://www.elperuano.pe/noticia/276073-minedu-premia-el-talento-estudiantil-y-docente-por-innovar-con-inteligencia-artificial-y-robotica-en-hackathon)
- **MINEDU Hackathon 2024** ("Robótica Educativa y Tecnologías Digitales – Edición Bicentenario", 2–4 Aug 2024, Huampaní). The teacher category went to an Arequipa team for **"Sessiontec: sesiones de aprendizaje en tu makerspace"**. The expert category went to Eduardo Vergaray and Graciela Asmat for **"Carton Bit"**. — [Andina](https://andina.pe/ingles/noticia-ministro-educacion-minedu-promueve-democratizar-ciencia-995756.aspx); [Diario Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/peru/ministerio-de-educacion-alista-concurso-de-robotica-y-tecnologias-digitales-noticia/)
- Offline AI tutors are a recurring hackathon theme. Examples: EduNest on Devpost (offline-first AI tutor, no prize shown); FutureFlux (Raspberry Pi 5 offline tutor; Myanmar national winner and 3rd regional at the AI Ready ASEAN Youth Challenge); ResilientEDU/Edunova at DigiEduHack 2025 (offline assistant fine-tuned on a grade 5–8 science curriculum). — [AngelHack](https://angelhack.com/?p=19858); [DigiEduHack canvas](https://digieduhack.com/challenges-images/PDF/Digi_Edu_Hack_Solution_Canvas_ResilientEDU_1.pdf); [Devpost example](https://devpost.com/software/we-re-cooked-gzh0xo)
- The Wadhwani AI for Education Hackathon (India, 5 Mar–1 Apr 2026) focused on prototypes for Indian classrooms. No winners were found. — [Reskilll blog](https://blogs.reskilll.com/?p=23)
- An IDB-backed Lima civic hackathon on city security had winners Rohirrim and Yachay Lima. It was not about education but shows the IDB–Lima challenge format. — [IDB blog](https://blogs.iadb.org/ciudades-sostenibles/es/innovacion-ciudadana-para-mejorar-la-seguridad-en-lima)

### Inferences
- MINEDU juries reward **in-classroom, low-cost, teacher-usable** artifacts (makerspace sessions, cardboard microcontrollers) more than SaaS. A Hack the Andes pitch that shows a teacher using it tomorrow with existing devices fits that taste.
- "Offline AI tutor" alone is now generic in global hackathons. Differentiation must come from Peruvian context and data integration (SIAGIE, Alerta Escuela, CNEB, multigrade, Quechua) and from evidence-backed mechanics such as level-targeted interaction.

## 6. Investor view: LatAm EdTech funding 2023–2026 and B2G viability in Peru

### Takeaway
Funding is depressed. Global edtech VC hit roughly decade lows in 2024 (about $2.4B) with a mild 2025 rebound (about $2.6B, secondary sources), and LatAm VC overall was flat at about $4.3B in 2025 against $16B in 2021. K-12 gets only about 10% of LatAm edtech investment, against 59% globally. Peruvian B2G edtech survives mainly on grants and private-school pilots. No documented path into MINEDU procurement was found.

### Cited Findings
- LatAm VC was about US$4.3B in 2025, similar to 2023 and below 2024's US$4.6B, against about US$16B in 2021. Early stage raised US$2.2B in 2025, the highest since 2022. — [Portafolio citing LAVCA](https://www.portafolio.co/emprendimiento/termino-la-era-del-dinero-facil-asi-cambio-el-negocio-de-las-startups-en-america-latina-497722); [LAVCA 2025 Trends in Tech](https://www.lavca.org/research/2025-lavca-trends-in-tech/)
- Only 10% of LatAm edtech financing goes to K-12, against a 59% global average (McKinsey-linked report). — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/peru/solo-el-10-de-la-inversion-en-edtech-en-latinoamerica-se-destina-a-la-educacion-escolar-noticia/)
- Global edtech VC was about $2.4B in 2024 (HolonIQ, described as a decade low) and about $2.6B in 2025 (+11%), both via secondary blogs. Q1 2025 was $410M, with "fewer deals, bigger bets". — [EdTech Hub library – HolonIQ Q1 2025](https://docs.edtechhub.org/lib/JFBLFFJF); [Voxbooster](https://voxbooster.com/es/blog/edtech-funding-statistics-2026/)
- In HolonIQ's LatAm EdTech 100, Brazil has 42% of companies, Mexico 18% and Argentina 11% (2023 list). Peru is not among the leaders. — [Mercado&Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/14/02/2024/noticias/brasil-tem-4-em-cada-10-edtechs-mais-promissoras-da-america-latina/amp); [HolonIQ LAC note](https://www.holoniq.com/notes/education-technology-in-latin-america-and-the-caribbean)
- A Tecnológico de Monterrey expert places Peru among the most active LatAm edtech ecosystems, alongside Colombia, Chile and Mexico. The TPrize co-funds 5 regional startups. — [Gestión edtech tag](https://gestion.pe/noticias/edtech/)
- Peruvian public funding: ProInnóvate / StartUp Perú non-reimbursable grants (S/ 50k–150k seed reported) and the PIMEN line. — [Ecosistema Startup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/tres-edtech-peruanas-financiadas-por-proinnovate-en-2026/); [Innovación Digital 360](https://www.innovaciondigital360.com/industria-4-0/cual-es-el-potencial-edtech-peruano); [Entiendo.ai](https://ecosistemastartup.com/entiendo-ai-edtech-peruana-con-ia-entra-a-venture-ready/)
- An IDB blog post exists on aligning LatAm edtech investment with learning, but the fetch failed (403), so its content was not verified. — [IDB blog](https://www.iadb.org/es/blog/educacion/edtech-en-america-latina-y-el-caribe-como-alinear-la-inversion-con-el-aprendizaje)

### Inferences
- A K-12 public-school product in Peru should pitch a **low-cost, grant-fundable pilot** path: ProInnóvate, IDB/World Bank-financed MINEDU programs, regional governments, or a UGEL. Investors will not fund an unproven B2G sale. Evidence (RCT-style metrics like Rori or ConnectEd) and near-zero marginal cost (WhatsApp, existing SIAGIE data) improve credibility.
- The 10% K-12 share signals both under-investment (opportunity) and difficulty (slow public buyers). Judges may reward a model with a B2B2C or free-to-teacher wedge.

### Gaps
- Specific MINEDU/Perú Compras/OSCE procurement rules for educational software, catalog listings, or startup-specific barriers: not found.
- LAVCA edtech-only figures for 2023–2026: behind a paywall.
- Documented LatAm K-12 edtech failures with causes: not found.
