# Infraestructura y servicios escolares en Perú: brechas, monitoreo y datos abiertos (corte: oct. 2026)

Note: the researcher ran about 17 search/fetch calls. Many figures come from press snippets surfaced by search, not from full primary documents. Each item is labeled with its year. Verify the starred (*) items against the primary source before putting them on a pitch slide.

## 1. Tamaño de la brecha de infraestructura (soles, estado de locales, servicios básicos)

### Takeaway
The official infrastructure gap estimate has grown from about S/100 mil millones (PNIE 2017) to S/152–172 mil millones (2022–2024 updates). Roughly half of the ~55,000 public school premises need total replacement. Only about 40% of premises had water, sewerage and electricity all connected to the public network (2022). The PNIE horizon ("al 2025") has now expired, and Minedu's own evaluation was published in Oct 2025.

### Cited Findings
- PNIE (2017) estimated the gap at about S/100,499 millones. The PNIE update with Censo Educativo 2022 data puts it at S/152,447 millones. — [Congreso, Comisión de Educación, presentación Minedu 2023](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/comisiones2023/Educacion/files/descentralizadas/comisión_educación,_juventud_y_deporte.pdf); [Minedu presentación Congreso 20.jun.2023](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/comisiones2021/pro-inversion/files/presentaciones/presentacion_minedu(20.jun.23)_(final).pdf)
- Gestión (19 Jan 2024), citing Minedu: 54.6% of public schools are in very bad condition and should be demolished. There are 55,436 public premises, the gap is S/164,000 millones, and 30,292 premises need integral intervention. Locales "en mal estado" that need partial replacement or reinforcement number 14,528 (about S/65,000 millones). Total replacement is defined as more than 70% of the roofed area requiring demolition.* — [Gestión 2024](https://gestion.pe/economia/ano-escolar-2024-el-546-de-colegios-publicos-en-el-peru-estan-en-muy-mal-estado-y-deben-ser-demolidos-minedu-pronied-escuelas-infraestructura-educativa-noticia/)
- El Comercio reports a gap of S/172,000 millones "al cierre del año pasado" (exact year not confirmed in the snippet, likely 2023 or 2024).* — [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/mas-de-un-millon-de-escolares-retornaran-a-colegios-en-riesgo-de-colapsar-noticia/)
- Historic figures. 2018: PRONIED said 27,400 schools (about 50%) needed demolition. 2019: 24% of schools were in good condition, 46% needed total replacement and 30% needed improvement or reinforcement. — [Andina 2018](https://andina.pe/ingles/noticia-mas-27000-colegios-deben-ser-demolidos-problemas-deterioro-700498.aspx); [Gestión 2019](https://gestion.pe/peru/peru-al-menos-26000-colegios-necesitan-ser-demolidos-por-deficiencias-en-infraestructura-minedu-noticia/)
- Seismic and collapse risk: 45% of school premises are at high risk of collapse, per the PNIE al 2025 (cited by Gestión).* — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/economia/cuatro-de-cada-cinco-proyectos-de-infraestructura-educativa-registran-cero-avance-noticia/)
- 2022 statement: the Education minister said closing the gap would take 20 years. — cited via search summary of [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=376335) (label: 2022, weak sourcing)
- Minedu's final evaluation of the PNIE al 2025 (Oct 2025, covering 2017–2025) found high relevance in design but only moderate coherence of the management model (about 70%). Gaps remain for Indigenous peoples and persons with disabilities. — [Repositorio Minedu, Evaluación PNIE](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/12501)
- Basic services, Censo Educativo 2022. Of 65,659 basic-education premises, 47% are connected to the public water network, 57% have sewerage and 82% have electricity. Only about 40% have all three. 70.8% of public premises (38,233) lack one or more of the three basic services, a share that has been stable for about 5 years. — [ESCALE, Alertas CE2022](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Bases%20de%20datos/A.%20Censo%20Educativo/2022/Presentaciones/Presentacion_Alertas_CE2022.pdf); [ESCALE EduDatos 46 servicios básicos](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Analisis/Edudatos/Edudatos%2046-servicios%20basicos.pdf) (the EduDatos 46 date was not confirmed and may be older)
- Puno (2023): only 21.2% of public basic-education schools have all three basic services (ComexPerú, using Minedu data). Puno DRE (Feb 2025): more than 3,700 of about 4,000 schools (about 93%) have deficient infrastructure, and only 205 are adequate. — [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=376335) (search summary)
- Internet, regional example: in La Libertad (2025), 2,150 premises (61.71%) lack internet access. — [Correo La Libertad](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/la-libertad/la-libertad-la-libertad-137-instituciones-educativas-sin-garantia-de-servicios-basicos-y-seguridad-noticia/)
- La Libertad (Mar 2026): more than 41% of schools are deteriorated enough that they should be demolished. — [El Búho 2026](https://elbuho.pe/?p=417192) (search summary)
- Slow execution: 4 of 5 educational infrastructure projects registered zero progress. Example: a Piura project with a S/14.3M budget had 0.1% accrued. — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/economia/cuatro-de-cada-cinco-proyectos-de-infraestructura-educativa-registran-cero-avance-noticia/)

### Inferences
- The gap grew rather than closed over the PNIE period. Each update raises the soles figure (costs, deterioration and better measurement all contribute). A pitch line that holds up: "around S/150–170 mil millones, and about half of public schools need to be replaced".
- Basic-services coverage changes slowly, so the CE2022 figures are a fair proxy for 2026. Regional disparities, such as Puno at 21%, are the most compelling story.

### Gaps
- No national Censo Educativo 2024 or 2025 percentages for water, sewerage, electricity or internet were found. The microdata exist on ESCALE but were not processed here.
- No official post-2025 successor plan to the PNIE was found. The "75% need replacement or reinforcement" figure was not confirmed.
- No consolidated national internet-connectivity percentage for schools was found.

## 2. Programa de Mantenimiento de Locales Escolares, obras paralizadas y Contraloría

### Takeaway
Maintenance funding is small, about S/296.6M nationally in 2026 (very roughly S/5,000 per premise on average). The money is deposited into the director's Banco de la Nación account, and directors report spending themselves (historically with poor compliance). Stalled works are a national problem: 2,572 paralyzed public works worth about S/43,163M (Sept 2025), most of them in local governments.

### Cited Findings
- RM 007-2026-MINEDU approves the Programa de Mantenimiento 2026 for up to S/296,602,800. Of that, S/2,000,000 goes to bicycles (Rutas Solidarias) and S/1,602,800 to monitoring services. It covers preventive and/or corrective maintenance. — [RM 007-2026-MINEDU (gob.pe)](https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/9260718/7599994-rm-n-007-2026-minedu.pdf)
- Regional allocations for 2026: Cajamarca S/33,284,111 for 6,420 premises (about S/5,184 per premise), Puno S/22,298,921 for 4,006, and Loreto S/21,038,120 for 3,895. — [El Peruano](https://www.elperuano.pe/noticia/287877-mejoraran-mas-de-55000-escuelas); [Exitosa](https://www.exitosanoticias.pe/actualidad/minedu-oficializa-lista-colegios-publicos-recibiran-servicios-mantenimiento-conservacion-link-n167347)
- Funds are deposited in a Banco de la Nación account under the name of the director or the person responsible for maintenance. Work follows the priority order set by the technical norm, and Minedu publishes an annex listing each school with its amount. — [Exitosa](https://www.exitosanoticias.pe/actualidad/minedu-oficializa-lista-colegios-publicos-recibiran-servicios-mantenimiento-conservacion-link-n167347)
- Earlier year: 2023 maintenance was S/295 millones. — [Congreso comunicaciones](https://comunicaciones.congreso.gob.pe/damos-cuenta/minedu-cuenta-con-s-295-millones-para-mantenimiento-y-reparacion-de-colegios)
- Historic audit (2011): the Contraloría found that 83% of the 40,503 directors who received more than S/103M under the maintenance program had not accounted for the funds. Findings included undocumented spending and funds used for other purposes. — [Panamericana (2011, old)](https://panamericana.pe/locales/97261-informe-revela-que-muchos-directores-no-dieron-cuentas-de-gastos-en-colegios)
- Paralyzed works (Sept 2025): 2,572 public works are paralyzed nationwide (health, education, transport), worth about S/43,163 millones. 71.8% belong to local governments. — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=764903); [Congreso](https://comunicaciones.congreso.gob.pe/?p=378434)
- Junín (as of 31 Dec 2024, INFOBRAS): 108 paralyzed works, 18 in Education. The main cause was breach of contract. — [Congreso, Comisión Educación, control político Junín](https://www.congreso.gob.pe/Docs/comisiones2024/Educacion/files/control_politico_7_-_junín.pdf)
- South of Peru: paralyzed works in 56 schools affected thousands of students. — [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=337541)
- Chiclayo (Jan 2025): the "Miguel Muro Zapata" school was demolished and the site abandoned, affecting 1,150 students. The Contraloría informed the Fiscalía. — [Congreso](https://comunicaciones.congreso.gob.pe/damos-cuenta/congresista-norma-yarrow-contraloria-informo-a-la-fiscalia-sobre-abandono-de-las-obras-del-colegio-miguel-muro-de-chiclayo/)
- La Libertad: 83 paralyzed works. The Contralor announced preventive control to reactivate 37, including school projects. — [Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/la-libertad/hay-83-obras-paralizadas-en-la-region-la-libertad-trujillo-peru-noticia/)
- 2015 baseline: INFOBRAS had 35,694 registered works, and the Contraloría identified 560 paralyzed works worth S/4,293M. — [El Búho 2015 (old)](https://elbuho.pe/archivo/2015/07/03/mas-de-550-obras-paralizadas-por-4-mil-300-millones-detecta-contraloria/index.html)

### Inferences
- About S/5,000 per premise a year cannot fix structural problems. It covers roofs, bathrooms and paint at best. Accountability rests on self-reporting by directors, which a hackathon tool could make public and verifiable, for example with before/after photos tied to the published per-school amount.
- The count of paralyzed works rose sharply, from 560 in 2015 to 2,572 in 2025, though the methodologies may differ.

### Gaps
- No recent (2022–2026) Contraloría national audit of maintenance-fund irregularities was found. The "Mi Mantenimiento" reporting platform was not verified: its name, URL and current status are unconfirmed.
- No national 2025/2026 count of paralyzed works specific to the education sector was found. This can be computed from the INFOBRAS data.

## 3. Alimentación escolar: Qali Warma → Wasi Mikuna → PAE

### Takeaway
After the Nov 2024 scandal (horse meat in Frigoinca canned goods, along with poisonings), Qali Warma was renamed Wasi Mikuna (2025). More poisonings followed in April 2025, and Wasi Mikuna was then extinguished and replaced by the Programa de Alimentación Escolar (PAE) in 2025. Sources conflict on dates, and the exact operating status as of Oct 2026 was not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Nov 2024: a former Frigoinca employee alleged that canned goods labeled as beef and supplied to Qali Warma since 2021 contained horse meat. She also alleged bribery of Qali Warma and Digesa staff. These are witness allegations. Recipient schools were in La Libertad, Áncash, Huánuco, San Martín, Amazonas, Cajamarca and Lambayeque. — [RPP](https://rpp.pe/peru/actualidad/qali-warma-denuncian-distribucion-de-carne-de-caballo-en-almuerzos-escolares-noticia-1597461); [La República](https://larepublica.pe/politica/actualidad/2024/11/10/carne-de-caballo-en-qali-warma-y-testigo-apunta-a-vocero-de-palacio-862560)
- The Fiscalía seized more than 27,000 boxes of adulterated canned goods in raids on 13 properties (La Libertad, Áncash, Lima). — [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-caso-qali-warma-fiscalia-incauta-mas-27000-cajas-conservas-adulteradas-1052518.aspx)
- Larvae were found in Qali Warma chicken cans in Jaén, Cajamarca (2024). — [Expreso](https://www.expreso.com.pe/actualidad/qali-warma-hallan-larvas-en-conservas-de-pollo-de-programa-de-alimentacion-cajamarca-jaen-noticia/1151943/)
- Government created "Wasi Mikuna" to replace Qali Warma. — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=728978)
- April 2025: about 120 children were poisoned in one week (Áncash, Amazonas, Piura). Wasi Mikuna also distributed milk that had been declared non-conforming under Qali Warma. — [Infobae 3 Apr 2025](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/04/03/alerta-por-wasi-mikuna-casi-100-ninos-intoxicados-en-ancash-amazonas-y-piura-en-solo-una-semana/); [Infobae 2 Apr 2025](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/04/02/wasi-mikuna-distribuyo-leche-que-fue-declarada-no-conforme-en-qali-warma-se-habia-ordenado-no-consumirla/); [Infobae Amazonas](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/04/03/wasi-mikuna-escolares-de-amazonas-presentan-sintomas-de-intoxicacion-luego-de-ingerir-sus-alimentos/)
- Piura: more than 100 children were poisoned, and the Fiscalía opened an investigation. — [El Tiempo](https://eltiempo.pe/local/fiscalia-investiga-caso-de-intoxicacion-de-mas-de-100-ninos-en-piura/)
- A Cuarto Poder report linked several cases to canned goods from Karsol SAC, a company with prior PRODUCE and OEFA sanctions. — [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=380590) (search summary)
- The MIDIS minister (Leslie Urteaga) admitted Wasi Mikuna was the same model as Qali Warma. MIDIS ended the product-purchase model, and the minister announced the program's extinction. — [Andina](https://andina.pe/ingles/noticia-midis-dispuso-extincion-del-modelo-compra-productos-para-servicio-wasi-mikuna-1022892.aspx); [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/267614-ministra-leslie-urteaga-anuncia-extincion-del-programa-wasi-mikuna)
- DS 006-2025-MIDIS extinguished Wasi Mikuna, and DS 008-2025-MIDIS created the Programa de Alimentación Escolar (PAE) under MIDIS (Gestión, 16 Aug 2025). — [CIP pronunciamiento Jan 2026](https://www.cip.org.pe/publicaciones/2026/enero/portal/pronunciamiento-2026001-presidente-capitulos-ccdd.pdf); [Gestión tag](https://gestion.pe/noticias/wasi-mikuna/)
- Transitional model: a cash-transfer modality to comités de madres de familia (Ayacucho, Piura, Cusco), with MIDIS and PMA (WFP) support. — [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/266362-ministra-urteaga-presento-nueva-modalidad-de-servicio-alimentario-de-wasi-mikuna-en-ayacucho)
- Coverage claim: the government guarantees school feeding for more than 4.2 million users. — [El Peruano](https://elperuano.pe/noticia/266007-midis-gobierno-garantiza-el-servicio-alimentario-escolar-a-mas-de-42-millones-de-usuarios)
- Conflict: Correo (May 2026) still reports spoiled "Wasi Mikuna" food in Piura, suggesting the name or operations persisted locally. — [Correo tag](https://diariocorreo.pe/noticias/wasi-mikuna/)

### Inferences
- Food-quality monitoring relies on school feeding committees (CAE) and post-hoc complaints. There is no public, school-level, real-time record of deliveries and incidents, which is an obvious hackathon opportunity.

### Gaps
- The exact PAE operating model and coverage for 2026, and any 2026 incident counts, were not confirmed.
- No Ojo Público or Contraloría audit specific to Qali Warma was retrieved in this pass.

## 4. Problemas recurrentes de inicio de año escolar (materiales, docentes, clima, colegios en mal estado)

### Takeaway
Every year the Contraloría's "Buen Inicio del Año Escolar" operation finds the same failures: work materials that have not arrived (70% of sampled schools in 2024), missing teachers, closed schools and dangerous infrastructure. In 2026, climate (a strong El Niño Costero, floods) added disruptions, including early school-year closure in Piura.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 operation (15 Feb–8 Mar): 395 public schools were supervised. In 70%, the communication and mathematics workbooks had not arrived by the start of the school year. — [Contraloría, gob.pe](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/contraloria/noticias/918845-contraloria-alerta-que-70-de-colegios-supervisados-no-recibio-material-educativo)
- 2024: 88% of schools lacked toilets for persons with disabilities (Contraloría operation). — search summary of Contraloría operation coverage ([Expreso](https://www.expreso.com.pe/actualidad/alumnos-corren-peligro-en-colegios-por-infraestructura-deteriorada-contraloria-hizo-inspeccion-y-encontro-ambientes-inhabitables-sin-agua-y-sin-servicios-ugel-minedu-ministerio-de-educacion-noticia/1267333/))*
- 2025 operation: a sample of 1,074 public schools, checking enrollment, material delivery and infrastructure. — [Correo Ica](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/ica/ica-contraloria-inicia-operativo-educacion-2025-en-colegios-publicos-para-garantizar-ano-escolar-noticia/); Piura findings: deteriorated infrastructure, lack of teachers, risk of school violence — [El Tiempo](https://eltiempo.pe/local/contraloria-identifica-infraestructura-deteriorada-falta-de-docentes-y-riesgo-de-violencia-escolar-en-colegios-de-piura/)
- Lima: 144 of 190 supervised schools had infrastructure problems, and 30 premises had uninhabitable spaces. — [Expreso](https://www.expreso.com.pe/actualidad/alumnos-corren-peligro-en-colegios-por-infraestructura-deteriorada-contraloria-hizo-inspeccion-y-encontro-ambientes-inhabitables-sin-agua-y-sin-servicios-ugel-minedu-ministerio-de-educacion-noticia/1267333/) (year not confirmed, likely 2025)
- 2026 operation: a sample of 1,166 public schools with 488 auditors from 26 regional offices. It covers infrastructure, furniture, maintenance, paralyzed works, safety, basic services and technology, with follow-up on implementation. — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/?p=787471); [Correo Ica](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/ica/contraloria-inicia-operativo-de-control-en-23-instituciones-educativas-de-la-region-ica-noticia/)
- 2026 results reported by Caretas (single press source, not verified against the Contraloría report): 349 schools lacked the teachers needed to open, 101 schools were found closed and 700 schools had serious infrastructure deficiencies.* — [Caretas, "Crisis educativa: del colapso de techos en 2025 al abandono administrativo en 2026"](https://caretas.pe/?p=796704)
- 2026 regional reports: deficiencies in 58 schools in Puno (71% lack a management plan), 35 in La Libertad, and Huancayo, where 75% of schools are in bad condition. — [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=413593); [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=415985); Huánuco — [Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/huanuco/alertan-deficiencias-de-infraestructura-mobiliario-y-otros-en-colegios-de-huanuco-noticia/)
- Classes started 16 Mar 2026: 6,225,000 students in 53,631 Educación Básica Regular premises. — [Infobae 27 Feb 2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/02/27/inicio-de-clases-2026-esta-es-la-fecha-oficial-que-fijo-el-minedu-para-alumnos-de-colegios-estatales/); [Infobae 25 Jan 2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/01/25/ano-escolar-2026-las-acciones-ejecutadas-por-minedu-para-asegurar-el-inicio-de-clases-para-mas-de-62-millones-de-estudiantes/)
- Climate 2026: floods and landslides killed 92 people with more than 23,000 displaced (as of 17 Mar 2026, OCHA). The Piura prefect proposed delaying classes. — [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/disaster/fl-2026-000038); [El Búho Mar 2026](https://elbuho.pe/2026/03/prefecto-de-piura-plantea-postergar-clases-ante-lluvias-del-nino-costero/); [ACT Alliance](https://actalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ACT-Alert-Peru-Floods-Emergency-2026.pdf)
- Minedu moved the end of the 2026 school year in Piura forward to 27 Nov because of El Niño, and brought forward the closure of 5 northern COAR. ENFEN expects a strong El Niño Costero into summer 2027. — [Caretas](https://caretas.pe/nacional/minedu-adelanta-fin-del-ano-escolar-2026-en-piura-por-fenomeno-el-nino-cuando-terminan-las-clases/); [Infobae 16 Jun 2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/06/16/minedu-adelanta-el-cierre-escolar-en-cinco-coar-del-norte-ante-riesgo-por-fenomeno-el-nino/)
- PRONIED's 2025 response: thermal comfort works (more than S/2.87M, 15 rural schools), emergency "domo" classrooms for 30 schools, and a digital technical-assistance platform (SIAT). — search summary ([Andina](https://andina.pe/ingles/noticia-pronied-implementa-medidas-para-dinamizar-expedientes-construccion-colegios-906584.aspx))*

### Inferences
- The Contraloría samples only about 1,100 of roughly 55,000 public premises (about 2%) once a year. The rest are unobserved until a crisis or press report, which is the core "monitoring gap" argument.

### Gaps
- No 2026 national figure on textbook or workbook delivery delays and no figure on contratación docente delays were found. No heat-related class suspensions for 2026 were found.
- The official consolidated 2025 and 2026 Contraloría reports were not retrieved.

## 5. Monitoreo ciudadano: herramientas existentes y cómo se reporta hoy

### Takeaway
Citizen monitoring is episodic and institution-driven: Contraloría volunteer "Monitores Ciudadanos de Control", annual Contraloría operations, Defensoría supervisions and press investigations. No public, school-level channel was found that lets a parent report a broken bathroom and track the response. Reports go informally through the director, APAFA, UGEL or press, or via generic complaint channels.

### Cited Findings
- Monitores Ciudadanos de Control are voluntary, unpaid citizens who watch public works and procurement in their district. They supported the school-start operations (Ica 2022: 55 monitors; Puno 2022: 100 monitors). — [Andina 2022](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-ica-contraloria-inicia-operativo-nacional-buen-inicio-del-ano-escolar-2022-880043.aspx); [Correo Puno](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/puno/puno-contraloria-de-la-republica-inspecciona-mas-de-300-planteles-educativos-de-puno-noticia/); [El Tiempo Piura](https://eltiempo.pe/local/piura-inicia-convocatoria-para-monitores-ciudadanos-de-obras-publicas-lmr/); [Correo Piura](https://diariocorreo.pe/edicion/piura/piura-contraloria-convoca-a-monitores-ciudadanos-de-control-vigilaran-obras-de-piura-supervision-de-contrataciones-publicas-noticia/)
- The Defensoría del Pueblo has asked PRONIED to act on the critical state of public schools (example: Chorrillos). — [Exitosa](https://www.exitosanoticias.pe/actualidad/defensoria-pueblo-pide-pronied-implementar-acciones-estado-critico-colegios-publicos-chorrillos-n145316)
- Students in Arequipa demanded that the Minister rebuild their schools, an example of protest-driven escalation. — [El Búho](https://elbuho.pe/?p=247175)

### Inferences
- A tool that lets parents, students or directors file geotagged incidents (photo, category such as water, bathroom, roof or electricity), linked to the padrón code and the school's maintenance amount, would fill a clear gap. It could export evidence to UGEL or Contraloría channels.

### Gaps
- Not verified: Contraloría's online denuncia channel URL and procedure, any Minedu or UGEL incident-reporting app, and the "Mi Mantenimiento" platform functions. Also unverified: Ojo Público investigations on school infrastructure or the feeding program, and whether the 2026 operation used Monitores Ciudadanos.

## 6. Datos abiertos utilizables en 36 h

### Takeaway
The best backbone is ESCALE's Padrón de Servicios Educativos (codes, location, levels; downloadable) plus the Censo Educativo microdata (local-level services and infrastructure; DBF/annual). Add the Mapa de Escuelas (SIGMED) for coordinates, INFOBRAS for works status, RM annexes for per-school maintenance amounts, and MEF Consulta Amigable for spending. Datosabiertos.gob.pe coverage of Minedu was not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- ESCALE Padrón page: a query tool that exports results (an email address is required) and a full "Descargar datos del padrón" download link. Columns reference "Censo educativo 2025". It links to the Mapa de Escuelas (sigmed.minedu.gob.pe). No file format is stated on the page. — [ESCALE Padrón](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/web/inicio/padron-de-iiee)
- Data dictionaries exist for each census year 2007–2024. The Padrón is distributed as .dbf (e.g. "Padron.dbf"). Fields include código modular, código de local, name, level, management type, address, centro poblado and ubigeo/district. Minedu warns that the location is approximate and should not be used as official boundaries. — [Diccionario marco censal 2024](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Bases%20de%20datos/A.%20Censo%20Educativo/2024/00_Diccionario%20marco%20censal%202024.pdf); [Diccionario 2023](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Bases%20de%20datos/A.%20Censo%20Educativo/2023/00_Diccionario%20marco%20censal%202023.pdf); [Diccionario 2022](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Bases%20de%20datos/A.%20Censo%20Educativo/2022/00_Diccionario%20marco%20censal%202022.pdf)
- Censo Educativo cédula 11 (local escolar) is the instrument for infrastructure and services, with historic cédulas published. — [Cédula 11 2008](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/documents/inicio/Bases%20de%20datos/A.%20Censo%20Educativo/2008/C%C3%A9dulas%20censales%20en%20PDF/C%C3%A9dula%2011_2008.pdf)
- INFOBRAS (Contraloría) is the national registry where entities report the progress of public works from start to finish. It is the source for counts of paralyzed works. — [El Búho 2015](https://elbuho.pe/archivo/2015/07/03/mas-de-550-obras-paralizadas-por-4-mil-300-millones-detecta-contraloria/index.html); portal: https://infobras.contraloria.gob.pe (export features not verified)
- The per-school maintenance list is published as an annex to the annual RM, here RM 007-2026-MINEDU. — [RM 007-2026](https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/9260718/7599994-rm-n-007-2026-minedu.pdf)
- PRONIED publishes quarterly transparency memos, for example on approved additional costs for works (Q4 2025). — [gob.pe](https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/9391119/6704845-adicionales-de-obras-iv-trimestre-2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- Join key strategy: the Censo Educativo "código de local" links the local-level census table (services, infrastructure) to the padrón (código modular and coordinates) and to the maintenance RM annex (likely by código de local). INFOBRAS joins only by name or ubigeo matching, which is fuzzy.
- A feasible 36h MVP: a map of every public school premises colored by missing basic services (CE), with its 2026 maintenance amount, nearby paralyzed works (INFOBRAS) and a citizen incident layer.
