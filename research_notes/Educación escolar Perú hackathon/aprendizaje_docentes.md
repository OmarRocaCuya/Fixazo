# Learning outcomes and teacher tooling in Peruvian schools (to Oct 2026)

## 1. Learning outcomes: ENLA / ECE 2022-2025, PISA 2022, learning poverty

### Takeaway
Only about 3 in 10 Peruvian 4th graders read or do math at the expected level (ENLA 2025: 32.7% reading, 30.0% math). Results drop sharply by secondary school (11.3% reading in 5th of secondary) and are much lower in rural schools (16.9% vs ~35.5% urban in reading) and in Amazonian regions such as Loreto (about 6% math). PISA 2022 placed Peru at 391 in math (down 9 points from 2018), 408 in reading and 408 in science, all below the OECD average.

### Cited Findings
**ENLA 2025** (applied Nov 2025, published May 2026; grades 4th primaria, 2nd and 5th secundaria)

Public schools, especially rural ones, improved in 4th-grade reading over 2024. 2nd secundaria math improved over 2023. Áncash and Ucayali stood out in primary, Puno in secondary. Moquegua, Tacna and Arequipa still lead. — [Minedu repositorio, ENLA 2025 resultados nacionales](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/12774); [gob.pe note](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minedu/noticias/1390772-enla-2025-se-mantienen-avances-en-lectura-y-matematica-con-mejoras-en-las-zonas-rurales)
- 4th primaria satisfactorio, national: 32.7% in Lectura and 30.0% in Matemática (IPE analysis reported by Infobae). La Libertad: 27.1% reading, 23.3% math. — [Infobae, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/10/02/peru-solo-tres-de-cada-diez-ninos-de-cuarto-de-primaria-logran-resolver-operaciones-matematicas-basicas/)
- Lectura satisfactorio: 32.7% in 4th primaria, 18.5% in 2nd secundaria, 11.3% in 5th secundaria. Cohort view: 34.8% of 4th graders reached satisfactorio in 2018; the same cohort scored 11.3% in 5th secundaria in 2025. The author warns the tests differ in design, so this is not a strict decline. — [De la evidencia al aula blog, 18 May 2026](https://delaevidenciaalaula.wordpress.com/2026/05/18/algunas-reflexiones-sobre-los-ultimos-resultados-enla-2025/) (secondary source citing the UMC report)

**PISA 2022**
- Math 391, down from 400 in 2018 (statistically significant). Reading 408 (401 in 2018) and science 408 (404 in 2018); neither change is significant. — [Minedu, gob.pe](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minedu/noticias/877904-pisa-2022-el-peru-mantiene-sus-resultados-en-las-competencias-de-lectura-y-ciencia)
- 34% of students reached Level 2 or higher in math, 50% in reading and 47% in science. Advantaged students outscored disadvantaged ones by 86 points in math. Peru is below the OECD average in all three. — [OECD PISA 2022 Country Note: Peru](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/pisa-2022-results-volume-i-and-ii-country-notes_ed6fbcc5-en/peru_3e71791c-en.html) (figures from search snippet; page returned 403 on fetch)

**Learning poverty (World Bank)**
- 56% of children at late-primary age cannot read with proficiency, adjusted for out-of-school children (4% out of school). This is the 2019 brief (older data). — [World Bank Peru Learning Poverty Brief 2019](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/669911571223458621-0090022019/original/LACLCC6CPERLPBRIEF.pdf)
- A 2024 Peru Learning Poverty Brief exists, but its figure could not be extracted. — [World Bank documents](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099060124164539924)

### Inferences
- The plateau at about 30-33% in 4th grade over 2023-2025, the collapse to about 11% by 5th secundaria, and the 2x urban-rural gap all suggest a lack of foundational skills that are never remediated. This fits a "teach at the right level / diagnose and group" tool.
- 2nd primaria math (11.2% in 2023) is the weakest point measured, so early numeracy is a strong target.

### Gaps
- No full official ENLA 2025 table by gestión, area and region for math; 2nd and 5th secundaria math percentages were not found (UMC PDF could not be fetched; SSL error).
- The current (2024) World Bank learning poverty figure for Peru was not extracted.
- The OECD average-gap figure for math conflicts across sources (104 vs 81 points).

## 2. Teacher reality: numbers, multigrado/unidocente, admin burden, training, salary, connectivity

### Takeaway
Peru has about 426,000 public teachers in basic education. Many rural schools are multigrado or unidocente, where outcomes are lowest. Salaries start at S/3,500.70 per month (30 h) in 2026. Quantitative data on administrative hours is weak and dated. The best figure is ENDO 2014: about 12 extra hours a week outside working time.

### Cited Findings
- The 2026 salary increase covers about 426,000 teachers in basic and technical-productive education (nombrados and contratados). — [El Popular, 17 Nov 2025](https://elpopular.pe/actualidad/2025/11/17/confirmado-minedu-revela-quienes-son-los-docentes-que-recibiran-aumento-salarial-en-2026-segun-la-nueva-normativa-337348) (secondary; no official total found)
- 2026 CPM scale: S/3,500.70 (1st scale, 30 h) to S/7,351.47 (8th scale). For 40 h: S/4,667.60 to S/9,801.96. Contracted teachers (Ley 30328) earn S/3,500.70 for 30 h. — [La República, 5 Jan 2026](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2026/01/05/sueldo-docente-en-2026-minedu-fija-nuevos-montos-para-cpm-y-profesores-contratados-207090); [Infobae, 2 Jan 2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/01/02/nuevo-sueldo-docente-2026-esto-es-lo-que-ganaran-los-maestros-de-la-cpm-y-profesores-contratados/)
- Bonuses: S/200 for unidocente and S/140 for multigrado (2026 scale). — [El Tiempo](https://eltiempo.pe/nacional/montos-de-nuevos-sueldos-de-los-docentes-nombrados-segun-escala-magisterial-2026/) (normative base not detailed)
- 2024-2025: 2,373 new teacher posts were created for schools that had one teacher, so 2,359 rural schools became polidocente incompleta. 4,773 teachers are in specialized multigrado training. — [Minedu, gob.pe](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minedu/noticias/1209569-gobierno-fortalece-educacion-rural-mas-de-2300-nuevas-plazas-docentes-convierten-escuelas-unidocentes-en-polidocentes); [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-educacion-rural-escuelas-unidocentes-se-convierten-polidocentes-gracias-a-2373-plazas-1037513.aspx)
- Multigrado unidocente schools were 29.6% of primary schools nationally (37.4% rural, 5.1% urban). This is OLDER data (INEI, around 2011). — [INEI cap. 2](https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1066/cap02.pdf)
- Unidocente schools are the clear majority of multigrado services. Satisfactorio in monolingual multigrado schools is only about 6-14%, lowest in unidocente schools. Cajamarca, Loreto and Piura have the most. Based on 2018-2019 censuses (OLDER). — [OBEPE, citing Miranda 2020](https://obepe.org/educacion-rural/educacion-rural-multigrado-en-el-peru-cifras-y-perspectivas/)
- ENDO 2014: teachers spent about 12 extra hours a week on educational tasks outside working hours (OLDER). — [CNE note](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/cne/noticias/504231-cne-presenta-publicacion-endo-2014-sobre-docentes-peruanos). ENDO 2021 overview: [Minedu ENDO 2021 presentation](https://www.minedu.gob.pe/politicas/docencia/pdf/endo-2021-presentacion-general.pdf)
- Internet access in primary schools: 38% (undated Congress document; treat as rough/older). — [Congreso, Educación Perú en cifras](https://www2.congreso.gob.pe/sicr/cendocbib/con5_uibd.nsf/5FA25F0D2231B886052583280083935F/$FILE/Informe_Educacion_Peru_en_Cifras.pdf)
- Infrastructure: 48.4% of public school buildings need full replacement (1.2 million students affected). In La Libertad, only 39.9% of public schools have water, sewage and electricity at once (Censo Educativo 2025). — [Infobae/IPE, Oct 2026](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/10/02/peru-solo-tres-de-cada-diez-ninos-de-cuarto-de-primaria-logran-resolver-operaciones-matematicas-basicas/)
- Minedu uses SIAGIE, SIMEX, SíseVe and other systems to verify school-management criteria in principal evaluation, which shows how much school work runs through these platforms. — [RVM 152-2026-MINEDU](https://evaluaciondocente.perueduca.pe/media/11787582727RVM-N-152-2026-MINEDU.pdf)
- Census data to compute exact 2025 totals: [ESCALE](https://escale.minedu.gob.pe/)

### Inferences
- Unidocente/multigrado teachers handle several grades plus all the admin alone, often with little or no connectivity. Tools must work offline, on low-end Android phones, and in mixed-grade groups.
- At about S/3,500 a month, paid tools aimed at teachers are unrealistic; the tool must be free or funded by the state or an NGO.

### Gaps
- No recent, specific measure of the hours spent on paperwork (SIAGIE, evidencias, planning) in basic education. ENDO 2020/2021 microdata on ESCALE could provide it, but it was not extracted.
- No official 2025 total for teachers, share of multigrado/unidocente schools, or school internet connectivity.
- No recent Minedu norm specifically reducing teacher paperwork was found.

## 3. Evaluación formativa / CNEB competency-based grading

### Takeaway
The CNEB requires formative, competency-based assessment: criteria, evidence portfolios, descriptive feedback, and literal grades (AD/A/B/C) entered into SIAGIE. Academic critiques say portfolios are often requested hastily and without a sound basis, but there is little quantitative data on the burden.

### Cited Findings
- Under the CNEB's formative approach, assessment looks at achievements, difficulties, progress and processes in each competency. The teacher must organize and file student evidence, keeping the pieces closest to the competency. — [Minedu guide, evaluación de los aprendizajes (CEBE)](https://www.minedu.gob.pe/educacionbasicaespecial/pdf/guia-evaluacion-de-los-aprendizajes.pdf)
- The AD/A/B/C scale (RVM 094-2020-MINEDU) is the only scale for grading competencies in EBR and recording them in SIAGIE. — [profeya.org](https://profeya.org/cneb) (non-official; verify against RVM 094-2020)
- Portfolios have sometimes been required hastily and without objectivity or sound practice. — [Redalyc article](https://www.redalyc.org/journal/6731/673171199001/html/)
- Academic analysis of formative assessment under the competency approach. — [Dialnet](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8054585.pdf)

### Inferences
- The likely pain point: turning observed evidence into criteria, a literal grade per competency and descriptive feedback for 30+ students (or several grades at once in multigrado), then entering it into SIAGIE. Assistance with rubrics, evidence capture and feedback is a plausible, defensible AI use case. Validate with teacher interviews.

### Gaps
- No survey quantifying the time teachers spend on formative-assessment paperwork or on planning (sesiones, unidades, carpeta pedagógica). SUTEP or teacher-union complaints were not found in searches.

## 4. AI in Peruvian classrooms: policy, PerúEduca, adoption

### Takeaway
Minedu has no binding AI guidelines for schools. Instead it promotes AI through courses (more than 87,000 teachers enrolled in 2025), PerúEduca tutorials and a guide to free AI tools. About 38% of school teachers report using AI, and about 90% want to learn.

### Cited Findings
- More than 87,000 teachers enrolled in a Minedu AI course designed by the Dirección de Formación Docente en Servicio, covering benefits, challenges and risks. — [La República, 13 May 2025](https://larepublica.pe/educacion/2025/05/13/capacitacion-en-inteligencia-artificial-para-87000-docentes-en-peru-en-que-consiste-y-como-impactara-la-educacion-atmpa-706394)
- PerúEduca published video tutorials on AI tools and, in June 2025, a guide of free AI tools organized by subject area ("19 herramientas"). — [Amauta en línea](https://amautaenlinea.com/blog/minedu-comparte-19-herramientas-con-ia-para-ensenar-y-aprender-para-docentes/); [GDP](https://www.gdp-pe.lat/2025/06/10/minedu-recomienda-ia-gratuitas-para-docentes/) (secondary)
- MentorIA (Kodea / Empresarios por la Educación, backed by Minedu) trained more than 1,300 teachers in 3 regions. It becomes a self-paced PerúEduca course from 2026. — [Kodea](https://kodea.org/la-inteligencia-artificial-se-ensena-en-escuelas-peruanas-mas-de-1-300-docentes-formados/); [EXE](https://empresariosporlaeducacion.org.pe/noticias/mentoria-peru-inteligencia-artificial-docentes-educacion-regiones-peru/)
- A Minedu course on curriculum planning with AI tools (2026) was reported by an unofficial site. — [mineduperu.org](https://mineduperu.org/curso-planificacion-curricular-ia-minedu-2026/) (unverified)
- A bill (Juntos por el Perú) proposes teaching AI in basic education from 2028; it is only a proposal. — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/peru/inteligencia-artificial-en-colegios-la-propuesta-congresal-que-busca-cerrar-brechas-digitales-noticia/)
- Neurometrics survey of 1,058 EBR/ETP teachers: 38.48% have used AI tools in daily practice, and 89.53% want to learn. — [Infobae, 25 Feb 2025](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/02/25/educacion-en-la-era-digital-estudio-revela-que-el-90-de-los-profesores-estan-interesados-en-usar-ia-en-sus-clases/); [Diario Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/peru/el-3848-de-los-profesores-usa-inteligencia-artificial-en-la-educacion-en-colegios-noticia/)
- 84% of Peruvians consider it essential that teachers be trained in AI (Ipsos). — [Infobae, 8 Jan 2025](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/01/08/que-podemos-esperar-de-la-inteligencia-artificial-en-la-educacion-peruana-para-este-2025/)
- A private product targets this niche: EducaTech IA ("IA para el docente peruano"). — [educatech.pe](http://educatech.pe/public/)

### Inferences
- Demand is high and supply is generic (ChatGPT plus tutorials). A CNEB-aligned, offline-tolerant tool for multigrado/EIB contexts fills a gap that generic tools miss. The official channel (PerúEduca) is a plausible distribution route.

### Gaps
- No official Minedu resolution or guideline on AI use in schools (data protection, student use) was found for 2024-2026.
- The Neurometrics survey method and sample representativeness are unknown.

## 5. Evidence-based interventions deliverable via tech (effect sizes)

### Takeaway
The strongest cost-effective evidence ("Great Buys", GEEAP 2023) is for structured pedagogy and teaching by learning level (TaRL). Phone tutoring plus SMS gives about 0.12 SD (Botswana, published), TaRL up to 0.53 SD with high fidelity, and adaptive software (Mindspark) about 0.59 SD in Delhi. Peru's own One Laptop per Child (devices without pedagogy) did not improve learning: a key cautionary tale.

### Cited Findings
- GEEAP 2023 "Smart Buys" (13,000+ studies screened, 400+ used): the "Great Buys" are (1) supporting teachers with structured pedagogy (lesson plans, materials, ongoing support) and (2) targeting instruction by learning level instead of grade. Also cost-effective: giving information on the benefits of education. — [World Bank feature, 21 Jun 2023](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2023/06/21/evidence-based-cost-effective-learning-education-improvements); [GEEAP brief, Oct 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/education/brief/global-education-evidence-advisory-panel)
- World Bank project uptake of smart buys: 12% (2015-19), 23% (2020-24), 68% (2025). — [CGD blog](https://www.cgdev.org/blog/are-aid-agencies-paying-attention-what-works-education)
- Botswana phone and SMS (Young 1ove / Angrist, Bergman, Matsheng): the combined treatment improved learning by 0.12 SD, or 0.89 SD per US$100, one of the most cost-effective interventions. The working paper reported 0.16-0.29 SD. — [Nature Human Behaviour 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-022-01381-z); [J-PAL working paper](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/sites/default/files/research-paper/working-paper_8778_Stemming-Learning-Loss-Pandemic_Botswana_Aug2020.pdf)
- Multi-country replication (5 countries; phone plus SMS): reported about 0.32 SD in math (secondary summary; verify). — [NBER w31208](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w31208/w31208.pdf); [summary blog](https://blueheadline.com/society/parenting/phone-tutoring-learning/)
- TaRL implementation fidelity (Botswana, 52 classes): the best delivery model averaged 0.53 SD, and effects vary with fidelity. — [EdWorkingPapers ai23-802](https://edworkingpapers.com/sites/default/files/ai23-802.pdf)
- Mindspark adaptive software (Delhi, Muralidharan, Singh, Ganimian, AER 2019; n=619): among the largest effects in developing-country education research. The World Bank blog cites about 0.59 SD in math. The Rajasthan in-school scale-up had mixed results: gains on independent tests, none on school exams. — [UCSD news](https://economics.ucsd.edu/events/news/MuralidharanAER2019.html); [World Bank blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/impactevaluations/technoskeptics-pay-heed-computer-assisted-learning-program-delivers-learning-results); [J-PAL PAL scale-up](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/node/8138364)
- Peru One Laptop per Child (randomized, about 21,000 students, 319 schools, 15 months): raised digital skills and some cognitive measures but did not raise academic achievement; small negative effects on timely completion. The authors recommend curriculum-aligned adaptive software and teacher training instead. — [IDB experimental evaluation (ES)](https://publications.iadb.org/publications/spanish/document/Evaluaci%C3%B3n-Experimental-del-Programa-Una-Laptop-por-Ni%C3%B1o-en-Per%C3%BA.pdf); [IDB blog](https://blogs.iadb.org/efectividad-desarrollo/es/hay-veredicto-un-laptop-por-nino-no-es-suficiente); [IDB long-term perspectives](https://publications.iadb.org/publications/spanish/document/Perspectivas-de-investigacion-se-benefician-los-ninos-del-uso-de-laptops-personales-a-largo-plazo.pdf) (OLDER, 2010-2012)
- UNESCO on TaRL from exclusion to implementation challenges: [UNESDOC](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000373668)

### Inferences
- The best fit for a hackathon tool is to make TaRL and structured pedagogy cheap for the teacher: a quick level diagnostic (ASER-style), automatic grouping across grades (a natural fit for multigrado), and ready-to-use leveled lesson scripts aligned to the CNEB. Optionally, low-tech SMS/WhatsApp practice for families. This targets the plateau of ~30%.
- OLPC Peru shows that hardware or content alone fails; the tool must sit inside the teacher's workflow.

### Gaps
- The meta-analytic effect size for structured pedagogy was not extracted (commonly cited ~0.2-0.3 SD; not verified here).
- No Peru-specific RCT of SMS-to-parents or phone tutoring was found (Peru-specific evidence such as MineduLAB evaluations was not searched; worth checking).

## 6. Indigenous-language (EIB) education gaps

### Takeaway
Peru lacks about 19,700 EIB-qualified teachers: of 76,228 teachers in EIB schools, only 56,753 are EIB teachers. Many bilingual posts are filled by Spanish-only teachers. Older ECE data show very low reading in native languages (for example, Aimara 9.8% satisfactorio in 2014), and materials are judged insufficient and monolingual in design.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 Minedu data at Foro Educativo: 76,228 teachers in EIB schools, but only 56,753 are EIB teachers, a gap of 19,745. Bilingual posts are often filled by Spanish-only teachers or by bilingual teachers without a teaching degree. — [La República, 20 Feb 2026](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2026/02/20/una-brecha-que-no-cierra-faltan-mas-de-19000-maestros-de-lenguas-originarias-en-peru-578000)
- An alternative figure: 72,701 teacher posts in EIB schools, of which 52,253 (72%) are held by EIB-trained teachers (Dirección de EIB). — [Servindi, Mar 2025](https://www.servindi.org/educacion-actualidad-opinion/10/03/2025/desafios-de-la-educacion-indigena-en-peru) (the bases differ from the La República figure)
- Defensoría del Pueblo Report 174 (via an academic source): 94% of teachers of Indigenous students had no EIB training, and 40% did not master the native language (OLDER, date unclear). — [CAAAP Amazonía Peruana](https://amazoniaperuana.caaap.org.pe/index.php/amazoniaperuana/en/article/download/766/510/1314)
- The state says it has materials in 18 languages. Studies find materials insufficient (Puno teachers, 2024), and most texts are designed from a monolingual, monocultural perspective. — [Revista Espacios 2024](https://www.revistaespacios.com/a24v45n06/a24v45n06p08.pdf)

### Inferences
- The overlap of rural, multigrado, EIB and Amazonian contexts is where outcomes are worst and teachers have the least support. A tool that helps a teacher who lacks full command of the language produce leveled reading material or assessments in Quechua or Aimara is high-impact. The risk is that LLM quality in low-resource languages (Amazonian languages especially) is poor, so a human-in-the-loop design and community validation are needed.

### Gaps
- No 2022-2025 national results of reading in native languages were found (the ECE EIB series appears irregular after 2018).
- No up-to-date count of materials by language or of delivery coverage.
