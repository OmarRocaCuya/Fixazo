# Habilidades 4C, metacognición y socioemocionales en escuelas peruanas: currículo vs. aula y pedagogías con evidencia (corte oct-2026)

Note on method: 15 tool calls (searches + fetches). Several primary PDFs (UMC full reports, Bruns & Luque Peru chapter, EEF site) were not retrievable (403 / binary / not indexed). Where only abstracts or secondary summaries were read, this is flagged.

## 1. CNEB 2016: competencias transversales, cómo se evalúan, problemas documentados y actualización 2024–2026

### Takeaway
The CNEB (RM 281-2016-MINEDU) frames these skills as competencies assessed formatively, but the available studies (mostly small qualitative theses/case studies) show teachers apply the competency-based assessment only partially and lack preparation on transversal competencies. In July 2026 Minedu formally opened a curriculum-update process (RM 393-2026-MINEDU) running to end-2027, with no published content changes yet and a politically charged context.

### Cited Findings
- The current curriculum was approved by RM N.° 281-2016-MINEDU; Minedu states it is reviewed periodically — [Minedu, ajuste curricular](https://minedu.gob.pe/curriculo/ajuste.php)
- Case study (PUCP, nivel inicial, Cercado de Lima): assessment "se aplica de manera parcial" despite teachers trying to adopt the CNEB formative approach; incoherence between discourse, instruments and practice (single case, not generalizable) — [PUCP repositorio](https://repositorio.pucp.edu.pe/items/162abea0-1ade-4a69-95d8-c3ebb543df18)
- UCV thesis (Lima, 2023) on four EBR teachers' strategies to develop and assess transversal competencies: finds a continuing need for teacher preparation on the nature of these competencies — [Repositorio UCV](https://repositorio.ucv.edu.pe/bitstream/handle/20.500.12692/105942/Ramos_VMI-SD.pdf?sequence=1)
- Revista Horizontes article frames the problem as how much Peruvian teachers know about competencies and which strategies they use, pointing to a training gap — [Revista Horizontes](https://revistahorizontes.org/index.php/revistahorizontes/article/view/2197/3452)
- A 2026 article on Peruvian secondary (search-snippet only; full text unreadable) reports "a substantial gap between the innovative rhetoric of the curriculum and its effective implementation in classrooms", attributed to traditional methods and content-focused assessment, and links it to weak PISA results in problem solving/reflective thinking — [Revista Horizontes 2001](https://revistahorizontes.org/index.php/revistahorizontes/article/view/2001/3338)
- **Update 2026:** RM N.° 393-2026-MINEDU (published El Peruano 19-Jul-2026) creates a temporary Comisión Sectorial (chaired by the Viceministro de Gestión Pedagógica; includes Minedu directorates, Secretaría de Planificación Estratégica and the CNE) to supervise and evaluate the update of the CNEB, plus four macroregional working groups (Norte, Sur, Centro, Oriente) with regional governments, universities, pedagogical institutes and unions incl. SUTEP; in force until 31-Dec-2027, ad honorem. The article does not mention transversal competencies or specify content changes — [El Peruano](https://www.elperuano.pe/noticia/300608-minedu-crea-comision-para-supervisar-la-actualizacion-del-curriculo-nacional-conoce-sus-funciones); [Diario Correo](https://diariocorreo.pe/peru/crean-comision-para-actualizar-el-curriculo-nacional-minedu-oficializa-el-proceso-modificador-noticia/)
- Political framing: Minister José Antonio Chang announced removal of "ideological" content from the curriculum; the reform is tied to the Plan Nacional de Educación 2026–2031 and is among the points of an interpellation motion — [Panamericana](https://panamericana.pe/politica/474321-minedu-cambia-curricula-retiraran-contenidos-considerados-ideologicos-evaluaran-docentes/amp); [Expreso](https://www.expreso.com.pe/?p=1317577); [Canal N](https://canaln.pe/amp/actualidad/vexler-apoya-presentacion-plan-nacional-educacion-2026-2031-n495033)
- IEP critique: rewriting the curriculum again without concentrating on teacher training "está lejos del cambio que necesita la educación peruana" — [IEP, "Otra vez el currículo"](https://criticaydebate.iep.org.pe/noticias/otra-vez-el-curriculo/?print=pdf)

### Inferences
- The documented gap is less "the curriculum lacks 4C skills" and more "teachers lack the training and tools to teach and assess them"; the 2026 update process addresses the document, not teacher capacity (IEP's point).
- Evidence on how transversal competencies (TIC; gestiona su aprendizaje) are assessed is thin and mostly qualitative/small-N.

### Gaps
- No CNE, GRADE or UMC national study specifically on the implementation of the two transversal competencies (TIC; gestiona su aprendizaje de manera autónoma) was found in the searches. CNE's annual "balance" reports and GRADE studies on CNEB implementation should be checked directly.
- Whether a 2024–2025 "ajuste curricular" was formally issued before RM 393-2026 could not be confirmed (the Minedu ajuste page exists but was not fetched).

## 2. Mediciones de habilidades socioemocionales en Perú (ENLA HSE, Young Lives)

### Takeaway
UMC now measures socioemotional skills (HSE) within ENLA (2023 report; 2025 report published 2026), finding associations of HSE with teacher emotional/academic support and respectful parenting; Young Lives Peru shows HSE gaps by poverty, gender and rurality and links them to later outcomes (observational).

### Cited Findings
- ENLA 2023 HSE report exists; a specific questionnaire was applied to 6.° primaria and 2.° secundaria; regional reports include an HSE section for 2.° secundaria — [ENLA 2023 HSE](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/10424); [ENLA 2023 Junín](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/10488)
- ENLA 2024 technical report includes a chapter on methodology for measuring HSE — [Reporte técnico ENLA 2024](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/11465)
- ENLA 2025 HSE (UMC, 2026) abstract: skills covered are self-regulation, empathy, assertive communication, collaborative conflict resolution and teamwork. Emotional well-being, prosocial behavior and positive attitudes to physical activity are associated with higher HSE; teachers' emotional and academic support is associated with stronger emotional regulation, relational, communication and teamwork skills; respectful parenting (dialogue, autonomy) associated with higher HSE, authoritarian parenting with lower HSE and more passive/aggressive conflict resolution. Some teachers believe HSE should be prioritized only for students with behavior problems; teachers report difficulty with certain tutoría topics. No numeric results on the record page — [ENLA 2025 HSE](https://repositorio.minedu.gob.pe/handle/20.500.12799/12776)
- Secondary (LinkedIn, unverified): in ENLA 2025, Amazonía and rural areas scored above the national result in self-regulation, teamwork, conflict resolution and empathy; Peru participated in OECD SSES rounds 2023 and 2026 — [LinkedIn post](https://pe.linkedin.com/in/alex-rios-c)
- Young Lives: gender gaps in self-efficacy widest among the poorest and rural; in Peru the overall gap narrowed by age 22 but poorest young women still report significantly lower self-efficacy — [Young Lives](https://www.younglives.org.uk/node/2022); [YL gender report 2023](https://www.younglives.org.uk/sites/default/files/2023-07/YL-GenderDifferencesReport-Jun23%20Final.pdf)
- Young Lives Peru (~700 children, ages 8–19): self-esteem at 12 and 15 negatively correlated with later risky behaviors; authors caution not causal — [YL Working Paper 154](https://www.gov.uk/research-for-development-outputs/psychosocial-competencies-and-risky-behaviours-in-peru-young-lives-working-paper-154)
- Peru cohort 8–22: higher "task effectiveness" skills associated with lower smoking, drug use, gang involvement — [Hossain & Jukes, LSE](https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/124441)
- Preschool attendance associated with higher agency and pride; for early starters (age 3–4) agency link persists to age 15 (GRADE/Young Lives, 2021) — [GRADE](https://grade.org.pe/publicaciones/the-relationship-between-access-to-pre-school-education-and-the-development-of-social-emotional-competencies-longitudinal-evidence-from-peru)
- Early circumstances shape HSE mainly via cognitive skills, which depend on family wealth and parental education — [YL Inequalities in Peru summary](https://www.younglives.org.uk/sites/default/files/migrated/YL-Summary-InequalitiesInPeru-May21-Proof02.pdf)

### Inferences
- The ENLA finding that teacher support correlates with HSE is consistent with HSE being teachable through classroom climate, but it is correlational.
- The teacher belief that HSE is "only for problem students" is a concrete, citable implementation barrier.

### Gaps
- No national numeric HSE scores (means, % by level) were retrieved; requires the full ENLA 2025 HSE PDF.
- No Peru measurement of critical thinking/creativity/collaboration as cognitive 4C skills was found (ENLA covers Comunicación, Matemática, Ciencia; PISA 2022 creative thinking — Peru participation not verified).

## 3. Práctica de aula: tiempo en exposición/copiado vs. aprendizaje activo

### Takeaway
Peru was among the countries in the World Bank Stallings classroom-snapshot study (2009–2012, >15,000 observations) showing low instructional time and heavy reliance on blackboard and seatwork; a Peru-specific breakdown and any Peru Teach results could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Stallings study: >15,000 teacher observations across Brazil, Colombia, Honduras, Jamaica, Mexico and Peru; method codes every 15-second snapshot for instruction vs. management, technique, materials, and share of students off-task — [World Bank blog, Berman/Bruns 2017](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/education/15-seconds-tell-you-about-classroom)
- Regional findings (Bruns & Luque, *Great Teachers*): low share of class time on instruction and low engagement partly explain weak learning; teachers rely on traditional methods (blackboard), 20–35% of time students do seatwork, little use of ICT — [PREAL/Inter-American Dialogue brief](https://www.thedialogue.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/FINAL-Time-on-Task-Brief-7-23-14-PDF.pdf); [Spanish brief](https://thedialogue.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/final-spanish-time-on-task-brief-7-23-14-pdf.pdf); [Great Teachers, OKR](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/8f5b687f-e4fd-5e5b-9243-0e2f8da019fe/download)
- Teach tool: tracks time on learning and students on task via 3 snapshots, plus quality areas including "socioemotional skills" and critical thinking; used in >30 countries — [Teach Primary Observer Manual](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/872291641201520569/text/Teach-Primary-Observer-Manual.txt); [World Bank 2024 doc](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099542305192520554/pdf/IDU-9ada5185-e4f9-4f96-8203-5c13082036e2.pdf)

### Inferences
- The regional pattern (blackboard, seatwork, low engagement) is the best available quantitative proxy for Peruvian practice, but should be presented as regional, not Peru-specific.

### Gaps
- Peru-specific Stallings percentages (time on instruction vs. the 85% benchmark; % copying from the board) not retrieved — need the Peru tables in *Great Teachers* (OKR handle 10986/20488).
- No Peru World Bank Teach application results found.
- No recent (2018–2026) representative Peruvian classroom-observation study on lecture/copying share found.

## 4. Qué construye estas habilidades: evidencia y tamaños de efecto

### Takeaway
Best-evidenced: metacognition/self-regulation (EEF +7 months, revised down from +8) and collaborative learning (+5 months); PBL with strong teacher PD showed gains of ~8–10 percentage points in AP pass rates in a 2021 RCT. Retrieval practice, Socratic questioning and debate were not verified in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- EEF Toolkit: metacognition and self-regulation = +7 months (down from +8 after adding new meta-analyses), "very high impact for very low cost based on extensive evidence" — [EEF technical appendix](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/metacognition-and-self-regulation/technical-appendix); [EEF blog on Toolkit updates](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/news/latest-updates-to-the-toolkit/)
- EEF collaborative learning = +5 months; defined as tasks in groups small enough for everyone to participate on a clearly assigned collective task — [EEF/Sutton Trust toolkit doc](https://stjamesjun.cumbria.sch.uk/serve_file/307081); [Structural Learning summary](https://www.structural-learning.com/post/eef-teaching-learning-toolkit-guide)
- Knowledge in Action RCT (Saavedra et al., USC, 2021; Lucas Education Research; ERIC ED616435): PBL + aligned curriculum + teacher PD in AP US Government and AP Environmental Science; PBL students ~8 percentage points more likely to score 3+ in year 1 and ~10 pp in year 2 (figures from press summary); gains held for lower-income students. Caveat: package effect (PBL + PD) vs. business-as-usual — [College Board brief](https://Apcentral.Collegeboard.org/media/pdf/project-based-learning-boosts-student-achievement-in-ap-courses.pdf); [The 74, authors' piece](https://www.the74million.org/article/analysis-project-based-learning-or-lectures-our-research-shows-pbl-helps-low-income-students-do-better-in-ap-classes-earn-college-credit/); [AEA registry](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/2189/history/199954/changes); [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/education-technology/project-based-learning)

### Inferences
- Methods with the strongest evidence (metacognition, structured collaboration, PBL with PD) are content-embedded and teacher-capacity-dependent, matching the Peruvian gap identified above (training).

### Gaps
- Retrieval practice, Socratic questioning, structured debate: not searched within budget. Known literature to verify (NOT checked this session): Adesope, Trevisan & Sundararajan (2017, Review of Educational Research) meta-analysis of practice testing; Abrami et al. (2015, RER) meta-analysis on critical thinking instruction (dialogue + authentic problems + mentoring most effective). Do not cite figures without verification.
- EEF padlock (evidence strength) ratings not confirmed from EEF site (403).

## 5. Harari "21 Lessons" (capítulo Educación) vs. críticas (Christodoulou, Willingham)

### Takeaway
Harari argues schools should shift from transmitting information to the 4Cs and the ability to handle change/reinvent oneself; cognitive scientists counter that critical thinking is domain-dependent and generic skill teaching transfers poorly, so knowledge remains foundational.

### Cited Findings
- Harari (Lesson 19, Education, in the "resilience" part) agrees with experts that schools should switch to teaching the four Cs (critical thinking, communication, collaboration, creativity), downplay technical skills and emphasize general-purpose life skills; the most important ability will be dealing with change/reinventing oneself (secondary summaries/reviews) — [Spirituality & Practice review](https://www.spiritualityandpractice.com/book-reviews/view/28653/21-lessons-for-the-21st-century); [Harvard Book Store](https://www.harvard.com/book/9780525512196); [Art History Abroad blog](https://arthistoryabroad.com/?p=8338); [SAJS article](https://sajs.co.za/article/view/5812/7332)
- Willingham: critical thinking (novel, self-directed, effective) is hard to teach because effective thinking differs by domain; explicit critical-thinking skill instruction only sometimes transfers; deep domain knowledge enables use of strategies; "Domain knowledge is a crucial driver of thinking skill" — [Willingham, "How to teach critical thinking" (NSW)](https://education.nsw.gov.au:443/content/dam/main-education/teaching-and-learning/education-for-a-changing-world/media/documents/How-to-teach-critical-thinking-Willingham.pdf); [Willingham, Knowledge and Practice (onlit)](https://onlit.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Willingham-Knowledge-and-Practice-The-Real-Keys-to-Critical-Thinking.pdf); [Chalkbeat 2026](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/05/28/ai-critical-thinking-schools-facts-knowledge-aft-randi-weingarten/)
- Willingham notes post-*A Nation at Risk* (1983) generic thinking-skills programs "were not very effective"; studying Latin did not improve general test performance — [Willingham NSW paper](https://education.nsw.gov.au:443/content/dam/main-education/teaching-and-learning/education-for-a-changing-world/media/documents/How-to-teach-critical-thinking-Willingham.pdf)
- Chalkbeat (May 2026, AI context): focusing on abstract skills disconnected from content risks de-emphasizing what supports critical thinking; frames against WEF "future-ready skills" — [Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/05/28/ai-critical-thinking-schools-facts-knowledge-aft-randi-weingarten/)
- Willingham practical advice: identify domain content students must know; plan which thinking skills recur across years — [KQED MindShift](https://www.kqed.org/mindshift/54470/why-content-knowledge-is-crucial-toeffective-critical-thinking)

### Inferences
- The two sides can be reconciled: Harari's goals (4Cs, adaptability) are widely shared; the dispute is about means. Evidence (sections 4–5) favors teaching thinking skills embedded in rich content (metacognition within subjects, PBL with content goals) rather than generic skills courses. For Peru, where CNEB already prioritizes competencies, the risk flagged by critics is "competencies without knowledge"; the risk flagged by observation data is "knowledge as copying without thinking".

### Gaps
- Christodoulou, *Seven Myths About Education* (2014): no source retrieved. Her core claims (myths that "facts prevent understanding", "21st-century skills/transferable skills", "teacher-led instruction is passive") should be sourced from the book or a review before use.
- Exact Harari quotations not verified from the book text.
